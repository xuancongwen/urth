# harrogath

Harrogath, the last fortress of the barbarians on the slopes of Mount
Arreat, and the mountain above it, up to the Worldstone itself. Reached
from the Pandemonium Fortress (`pandemonium`, room 3401): Tyrael's
portal sets you down on the portal terrace (3500), and north of it the
town. The end of the chain; nothing leads on. The town, from the
terrace to the great gate, is safe, all but the elder's house.
Everything inside the mountain (the ice caves, the temple halls, the
keep) is dark, so bring a light.

Levels, roughly in walking order: demon imps 50 (Shenk whips up more
of them), siege beasts 51, Dac Farren 51, Shenk the Overseer 52
(boss; his whip), Eldritch the Rectifier 53, frozen horrors 53 (chill),
snow yetis 54, Frozenstein 55 (boss; Frozenstein's grip and the shard of black
ice), abominables 56, the three Ancients 57 (Talic, Madawc and Korlic,
a group fight on the summit: they assist one another; the Immortal
King's war-gear), minions of destruction 58, the throne waves 58
(Colenzo the Annihilator, Achmel the Cursed, Bartuc the Bloody, Ventar
the Unholy), Lister the Tormentor 59 (Bul-Kathos' Tribal Guardian), and
Baal, Lord of Destruction 60 (the final boss, 32000 health, two swings
a round, a freezing touch that slows, a crackle of hoarfrost; carries
Tal Rasha's Guardianship, Death's Fathom, and the Worldstone
shard). Off the main road, through Anya's portal: defiled warriors 54
(perfect skulls), Pindleskin 55 (his skull), and Nihlathak, the
betrayer 56 (boss; his staff).

Named health: Dac Farren 10000, Shenk 16000, Eldritch 14000,
Frozenstein 18000, Pindleskin 16000, Nihlathak 40000, each Ancient
20000, each throne captain 14000, Lister 40000, Baal 32000.

Attack on sight: the demon imps, Eldritch the Rectifier (who sees
hiding and sneaking players at his waystones), frozen horrors (hidden,
standing still as drifts until they lurch out), snow yetis, defiled
warriors, and minions of destruction.

Nihlathak the elder (56; 16000 health, leeches) sits among the empty
chairs of the council in town (3505). He is not peaceful and his house
is not safe: attacked, he fights, and he wears the seal of the Elder
Council (56; Intelligence, Wisdom, sees the hidden). His
other self waits in the Halls of Vaught.

Uniques: Shenk's whip (52; punishes the staggered, burns, Strength),
the seal of the Elder Council (56; on Nihlathak the elder;
Intelligence, Wisdom, sees the hidden), Pindleskin's skull
(55; thorns, last stand, Constitution), Frozenstein's grip
(55; chills, Intelligence and Strength), Nihlathak's staff (56; corpse
explosion, leech, Intelligence and Wisdom, necromancy stronger, mana), Immortal King's Stone
Crusher (57; crits, crushing damage, Strength), Immortal King's Soul
Cage (57; less damage taken, last stand, Constitution), Immortal King's
Will (57; steady, Strength and Wisdom), Bartuc's Cut-Throat (58; a
claw that leeches and crits, Dexterity), Bul-Kathos' Tribal Guardian
(58; burns and poisons, Strength and Constitution), Bul-Kathos' Sacred
Charge (59; forged only, see Larzuk), Tal Rasha's Guardianship (60;
less damage taken, soaks, Intelligence and Wisdom), Death's
Fathom (60; held; skill cooldowns a round shorter, sees the hidden,
Intelligence), the Worldstone shard (60; held, regeneration,
Constitution). The Immortal King's pieces are a set in name; each
stands alone. Common finds: Harrogath militia spears on the imps,
siege beast war harnesses, horror-hide hoods, yeti-fur mantles,
defiled warriors' falchions, frost-rimed greaves on the abominables,
hellforged vambraces on the minions; and, from corpses only, a
keep-forged greatsword (60) and a Worldstone Keep breastplate (59).

Merchants (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- Larzuk (3502): Harrogath war axe, barbarian war helm; sockets a
  perfect skull into one of his war axes (axe, skull, and 1000 silver);
  forges Bul-Kathos' Sacred Charge around the Worldstone shard (shard
  and 5000 silver).
- Malah (3503): super healing potion, Malah's thawing potion.
- Anya (3506): Harrogath fur boots; a red portal scroll for the shard
  of black ice Frozenstein carries. The scroll is the key to the red
  portal behind her stall (east, locked, pickproof), which opens on
  Nihlathak's Temple (3520).

Qual-Kehk (3504), on the wall above the gate, teaches berserk,
whirlwind and sunder.

Quest item: 3516, a scroll of resistance (planted by questmasters
only).
