# wayreth

Wayreth Forest and the Tower of High Sorcery, seat of the Red Order
(conjuration and illusion; RULES 6.1). Levels 12 to 18. Reached east from
the Haven Road (6112).

The forest turns travellers about: from the edge (6200) the right way is
east, east, east, east, following the trees that lean toward the dawn
(6201, 6204, 6206, 6207). The wrong turns end in a hollow with a twisted
dryad (13), a ring of standing stones with a stone guardian (14), and a
thorn thicket where a renegade mage (15) holds the Conclave summons, the
key to the Tower's bronze gate (pickproof). Wolves of the moving wood
(12) prowl the paths. Everything on the way attacks from level 1.

Inside, safe: the courtyard, the Hall of the Conclave, the library (the
Red librarian teaches the Red Order's school spells to its sworn
specialists), and the robing room (the robe-keeper sells the red robe
and the red staff). North of the hall is the Test: yourself (14), a
fire elemental (15), and the shade of Fistandantilus (17, for a grey
ring). Past them, the Chamber of the Test (6217, `order: red`), where
Justarius (22, peaceful) hears the oath: `swear conjuration` or `swear
illusion`, at level 10 and 15 Intelligence.
