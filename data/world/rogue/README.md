# rogue

The Rogue Encampment, where the Sisters of the Sightless Eye hold out
behind a palisade after their monastery fell to Andariel: the camp
itself, the Blood Moor and the Den of Evil, the Cold Plains and the
Burial Grounds, the Stony Field and the Cairn Stones, the Dark Wood
and the Tree of Inifuss, the Black Marsh and the Forgotten Tower, the
Tamoe Highland, and the monastery from its gate down to the catacombs.
Diablo II, Act I, the sequel to Tristram. Reached east from Tristram's
north road (diablo room 1300), on the road east out of Tristram
(3100). Warriv's caravan (3101) waits on the camp's east side, where a
track runs east to Lut Gholein's desert road (`lutgholein`, 3200).

The encampment (3101 to 3107) is safe; the road outside the gate
(3100) is not. The Den of Evil, the
mausoleum, the tower cellars, the jail, and the catacombs are dark;
the monastery's cloisters and cathedral are lit.

Levels, roughly in walking order: fallen and quill rats 22 (the Blood
Moor), zombies 23 (the Den, and Blood Raven's raised dead), Corpsefire
23 (the Den), Bishibosh 24 (the Cold Plains; raises fallen), Blood
Raven 25 (the Burial Grounds; raises zombies), carvers 25 (the Stony
Field, the Cairn Stones, the Black Marsh), Rakanishu 26, brutes 26 and
Treehead Woodfist 26 (the Dark Wood), ghosts 27 and the Countess 27
(the Forgotten Tower), moon clan goatmen 27 (the highland, the gate,
the outer cloister), the Smith 28 (the Barracks), dark spearwomen 28
(the jail, the inner cloister, the cathedral), Bone Ash 29 (the
cathedral), ghoul lords 29 (leech; the catacombs), and Andariel 30.

Boss: Andariel, the Maiden of Anguish (30) in her lair (3129): 3000
health and a venom that stacks. She wears Andariel's Visage and wields the Spineripper.
Named minibosses: Corpsefire (23, 900; a burning touch), Bishibosh
(24, 1000; fireballs, raises fallen; the Torch of Iro), Blood Raven
(25, 1200; dodges, raises zombies; Blood Raven's Charge), Rakanishu
(26, 1200; lightning; the Gull), Treehead Woodfist (26, 1300; soaks
blows; Bloodfist), the Countess (27, 1400; leech; her runes), the
Smith (28, 1500; crits, soaks blows; the Horadric Malus, Sigon's Gage
and Sigon's Shelter), Bone Ash (29, 1600; frozen bone, soaks blows;
the Nokozan Relic).

Uniques: Blood Raven's Charge (25; a bow whose arrows burst, crits,
Dexterity), the Torch of Iro (24; a wand that ignites and leeches,
Intelligence), the Gull (26; a lucky dagger, opportunist, Charisma and
Dexterity), Bloodfist (26; gloves, steady, Constitution and
Strength), Sigon's Gage (28; gauntlets, Strength and Dexterity),
Sigon's Shelter (28; a breastplate, defense, Constitution), the
Nokozan Relic (29; an amulet that soaks blows, steadies, and sees the
hidden), Andariel's Visage (30; a helm that leeches, Strength and
Constitution), the Spineripper (30; a venomous dagger that crits and
leeches), the Horadric Malus (28; a hammer, Strength). Gheed's
Nagelring (24; thorns, Dexterity and Charisma) and Gheed's Fortune
(26, held; Charisma and Wisdom). Runewords: Stealth (27; light body
armour, dodge, soak, Dexterity) and Lore (27; a helm, Intelligence and
Wisdom, sees the hidden). Charsi's imbued blade (30; crits, hits
harder, Strength).

The Countess carries one each of four runes, Tal, Eth, Ort, and Sol;
they are what Charsi and Akara bind into Stealth and Lore. Common
finds: a fallen's hand axe, grave-mud boots on the zombies, a carver's
blade, brute-hide jerkins, moon clan poleaxes, corrupted rogues'
spears, bone vambraces on the ghoul lords; and from kills, cloister
war swords, cathedral splint mail, Sisterhood war bows.

Merchants (`list`, `buy`, `sell`) and trainers:

- Akara (3100, her tent 3104): healing potion 30; an Ort rune and a
  Sol rune plus 200 silver for Lore; caster gear, a robe of the
  Sightless Eye (3135; 30, Wisdom, mana, a faith shield) 2200 and a
  rosary of the Sightless Eye (3136; 30, divine spells stronger, mana
  in a fight, Wisdom) 2000. She also teaches dodge and fast
  healing, and to the Good god's apostles Smite and Prayer of Healing.
- Kashya (3101, the captain's watch 3107): teaches trip and hamstring.
- Warriv (3104, the caravan 3101): healing potion 35, mail hauberk 400.
- Charsi (3102, the forge 3105): Rogue long bow 400, broad axe 450,
  mail hauberk 380, kite shield 360; a Tal rune and an Eth rune plus
  250 silver for Stealth; the Horadric Malus (the Smith, 3124) plus
  500 silver for Charsi's imbued blade.
- Gheed (3103, his wagon 3106): the Nagelring 2000, Gheed's Fortune
  2500.

Flavie the scout (3105, 24) keeps watch on the road outside the gate
(3100), not in the camp: she does not start a fight, but she can be
killed, and she carries a Rogue long bow and 90 silver.

Quest item: 3101, the scroll of Inifuss (planted by questmasters only).
