# shire

The Shire in the last quiet days before Frodo leaves: Hobbiton, Bywater,
the Water and the Mill, Farmer Maggot's fields, the Woody End, and the
edge of the Old Forest. Levels 1 to 10, with the Black Rider (9) on the
road through the Woody End and Old Man Willow (10, tough) by the
Withywindle. Reached through the west door of the Undercroft (room 6),
which opens as an old cellar door in the bank of the Bywater Road (400).
Hobbiton's lanes and Bywater are safe. Bagshot Row, the step of Bag End,
and Maggot's kitchen are not: the Gaffer (10; his stick and pipe-weed),
Lobelia (8; her umbrella), Ted Sandyman (6; his purse), and Farmer
Maggot (12; mushrooms) can be killed. The two Old Forest rooms
are dark. Beyond the Withywindle, east along the bank, the House of Tom
Bombadil (424) is safe.

Folk met on the walk: Gandalf the Grey (20) on his cart at the edge of
the Party Field (406); Rosie Cotton (6) serving in the Green Dragon
(409); Merry Brandybuck (7) waiting with a pony at the end of Bamfurlong
Lane (415); Frodo (8), Sam (8), and Pippin (6) hiding from the Riders in
the Woody End (418); and Tom Bombadil (20) and Goldberry (18) at home
(424). Those with something to trade or teach are peaceful. Merry (his
weskit and purse), Sam (a cast-iron frying pan and a purse), and
Pippin (a party cloak, mushrooms, a purse) are not: they fight back.

Merchants and trainers:

- Gandalf the Grey (406): a Gandalf firework 15s.
- Rosie Cotton (409): a pint 2s, a seed-cake 3s, a twist of pipe-weed 8s.
- Frodo Baggins (418): Thror's map for the letter to Mr. Underhill that
  lies under the door of Bag End (405), Gandalf's letter he never got.
- Tom Bombadil (424): a barrow-blade of Westernesse for a splinter of
  willow heartwood from the split trunk of Old Man Willow (422).
- Tom Bombadil (424) also sells caster gear, a circlet of river-reeds
  (437; 15, Wisdom, mana) 600s and a willow-wood staff (438; 20, the
  Neutral god's spells stronger, mana in a fight, Wisdom) 1000s, and
  teaches the Neutral god's Entangle, Barkskin, and Call Lightning to
  its apostles.
- Goldberry (424) teaches fast healing and second wind; the Bree ruffian
  in the Green Dragon teaches trip.

Notable items: the Black Rider wields a Morgul-knife (poison and chill)
and wears the torn black cloak (sees the invisible); Old Man Willow keeps
the One Ring (a plain gold ring, unidentified: invisibility, slow
regeneration, but 15% more damage taken and -2 wisdom) and Sting
(finesse, sees hidden things); Bilbo's mithril shirt (soak, extra
defense) is on the Black Rider; the ruffian on the East Road carries
Bullroarer's club (execute) stolen from Tuckborough. Thror's map
(held: sees hidden things, Intelligence, Wisdom) comes from Frodo, and
the barrow-blade (sees the invisible, crits, Strength, Dexterity) from
Tom Bombadil. Ordinary finds: a
belt (401), party cloak (404), party ring (406), pot-lid shield (403),
moleskin breeches (411), woodcutter's axe (408), frying pan in Maggot's
kitchen (417), Big Folk boots on the ruffian, an elven lantern in the
Woody End (418), and an Old Forest quarterstaff under the eaves (421).
