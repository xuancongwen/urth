# pandemonium

The Pandemonium Fortress, Heaven's last bastion at the edge of Hell,
and the Hell beyond its gate: the Outer Steppes, the Plains of Despair
and Izual's fallen star, the City of the Damned, the River of Flame and
the Hellforge, and the three outer seals of the Chaos Sanctuary. Diablo
II, Act IV. Reached up from the red portal in Mephisto's chamber in
Kurast (`kurast` room 3301, whose `down` exit opens on the portal court,
3400). The fortress's four rooms are safe; everything past the gate is
Hell, and dark, so buy a lamp from Jamella. North of Tyrael's hall
(3401) a blue portal opens on Harrogath (`harrogath` room 3500).

Act IV ends here at the Chaos Sanctuary's outer seals. The Sanctuary's
doors stay shut: the Lord of Terror himself waits where he first rose,
in Tristram's depths (`diablo` room 1328), and is not in this area.

Levels, roughly in walking order: corpulents 44 (the spitters' hollow),
doom knights 45 (the causeway and the bone field), undead soul killers
45 (wander, dodge), abyss knights 46 (fire), Izual 47 (boss), pit lords
47 (demon horns), venom lords 48 (a festering breath; demon horns),
storm casters 48, Hephasto the Armorer 49 (boss), tainted ones 49,
oblivion knights 50, the Grand Vizier of Chaos 50, Lord De Seis 51, and
the Infector of Souls 52 (the final boss).

Doom knights, undead soul killers, venom lords, tainted ones, and
oblivion knights attack on sight. The soul killers lie hidden in the
ash, and the oblivion knights see anyone hiding or sneaking.

Bosses:

- Izual, the fallen angel (47) in the fallen star (3410): 18000 health;
  a chilling cold strike, a frost aura, regeneration. Carries Izual's
  broken feather.
- Hephasto the Armorer (49) at the Hellforge (3416): 14000 health; soaks
  blows. Wields the Hellforge Hammer.
- The Grand Vizier of Chaos (50) at his seal (3419): 24000 health; two
  swings a round, a burning aura, regeneration; storm casters attend him. Holds his tome
  and carries a chaos seal fragment.
- Lord De Seis (51) at his seal (3420): 14000 health; two swings a
  round, thorns; oblivion knights stand with him. Wears his oathbound
  plate and carries a chaos seal fragment.
- The Infector of Souls (52), the final boss, at the last seal (3421):
  25000 health; a festering touch, dodge, leech;
  venom lords crouch by him. Wields Doombringer, wears his flayed hide,
  and carries the last chaos seal fragment.

Scroll: Izual also carries a scroll of holy word (3434), the Good god's
found spell; Tyrael's look points to it.

Uniques: Izual's broken feather (47, held; dodge, Dexterity and Wisdom,
a frost aura), the Hellforge Hammer (49; a two-handed maul that ignites
and crits, Strength), an amulet of the perfect skull (49; leech,
regeneration, Constitution; from Halbu), the Grand Vizier's tome (50,
held; Intelligence and Wisdom, steadiness, sees the invisible), De
Seis's oathbound plate (51; less damage taken, thorns, Constitution,
last stand), the Infector's flayed hide (52, shoulders; regeneration,
soaks blows, a pestilent aura, Constitution), Doombringer (52; a long
sword that burns and poisons, leeches, hits harder), Tyrael's Might
(52; plate that weighs nothing: less damage taken, Strength, Dexterity
and Constitution, dodge, sees the invisible; from Jamella). Common
finds: gore-crusted hide jerkins on the corpulents, doom knights'
blackened greatswords and visored helms, soul killers' bone daggers,
abyss knights' runed gauntlets, pit lords' cleaving axes, venom lords'
glaives, storm casters' cuffs and, on two of them, a storm caster's orb
(3435; 48, held, evocation stronger and surging, Intelligence), tainted ones' bone flails, oblivion
knights' greaves; and from kills only (flagged drop), ash-walker boots
47, a charred war scepter 50, a hellforged war maul 51, a hell-plate
cuirass 52, a fiend-bone helm and hell-iron vambraces 53.

Merchants (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- Jamella (3401, her alcove 3402): a vial of angelic healing 60, a lamp
  of Pandemonium 60, a prismatic amulet 4500, a ring of the heavens
  4500; three chaos seal fragments plus 15000 silver for Tyrael's
  Might; caster gear, a luminous vestment (3436; 40, Wisdom, mana,
  healing given comes back, a faith shield) 4200 and a holy symbol of
  the Arch-Angels (3437; 50, Wisdom, divine spells stronger, healing
  given comes back, mana in a fight) 8000.
- Halbu (3402, his forge 3403): a dwarven war axe 1400, a dwarven plate
  hauberk 1600; two burning demon horns plus 800 silver for a pavise of
  Pandemonium; the Hellforge Hammer plus 400 silver for an amulet of the
  perfect skull; a Hellforged flanged mace (3438; 50, Wisdom, the Good
  god's spells stronger, a faith shield, mana) 7800. He also teaches
  sunder.

Trainers: Tyrael (3400, his hall 3401) teaches rescue and second wind,
and to the Good god's apostles Smite and Prayer of Healing;
Hadriel (3403, the gate 3404) teaches shield block and riposte. Both
are peaceful and tell the way.

Quest item: 3433, a shard of the Soulstone (planted by questmasters
only).
