# gotham

Gotham City, after Batman: police headquarters under the Bat-Signal,
a rain-slick Midtown street and Crime Alley, the bridge to the Narrows,
a tenement full of the Scarecrow's fear gas, Ace Chemicals, the Iceberg
Lounge, the docks, and Arkham Asylum with every cell door open. Levels
4 to 8, with the Joker at 9: an early levelling area, crowded rooms, a
two-minute reset, and loot that is mostly what the drop rolls give.
Reached east from the Hall of Flickering Windows (room 13, north of
the Long Hall) through the fire door onto the steps of GCPD
headquarters (4100).

Rising from the entrance: street thugs (4) in Midtown, the alley, and
on the bridge; muggers (4) in Crime Alley; Falcone enforcers (5) on the
bridge and the docks; Joker goons (6) on the Narrows corners and all
through Ace Chemicals; fear-gassed thugs (6) in the tenement;
Penguin henchmen (6) in the lounge and on the docks; escaped Arkham
inmates (7) from the ferry landing to the cell block; Titan brutes (8)
in the cell block and the ward. Muggers, goons, fear-gassed thugs, and
inmates attack on sight.

Boss: the Joker (9, `boss`, 4113), with Harley Quinn (8) beside him.
Named: the Penguin (7, the Iceberg Lounge 4108) and the Scarecrow (8,
the tenement 4105).

Uniques, from loot tables (not worn, so not seen before the fight):
Harley Quinn's mallet (Harley, one time in four; two-handed mace,
blunt, a BONK proc, Dexterity) and the Joker's lapel flower (the
Joker, one time in five; neck, an acid-squirt proc on every swing,
Intelligence).

Common finds: nothing is carried. Every kill may roll one piece of
plain drop gear, one to three at each level from 3 to 9: tire iron and
cheap trench coat (3); flick knife and battered fedora (4); snub-nosed
revolver and black leather gloves (5); tommy gun and pinstripe
waistcoat (6); steel crowbar, SWAT helmet, GCPD riot shield (7); pump
shotgun, kevlar vest, combat boots (8); orderly's nightstick and
armoured riot suit (9).

Commissioner Gordon (peaceful) waits on the steps (4100), which are
safe.
