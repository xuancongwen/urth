# deathstar

The Death Star, from Star Wars (A New Hope): the Empire's battle station,
seen from the inside by someone who arrived in a smuggler's hidden
compartment. Docking Bay 327 and the freighter in it, the corridors and
the fighter hangar, the detention level (Detention Block AA-23, cell
2187, the interrogation chamber, and trash compactor 3263827), the
command corridor and the conference room, the maintenance shafts down to
the tractor beam terminal, the reactor shaft with its bridge retracted,
superlaser fire control and the overbridge, the Royal Guard, the
meditation chamber, and the Emperor's throne room above the guards, and
up on the surface the turbolaser battery, the
trench, and the exhaust port. Levels 15 to 55. Reached south from hub
room 10 in `start`, which opens onto the docking bay (2300).

Bosses: Darth Vader (55) in his meditation chamber, with 26000 health,
heavy armour, a soak, a Force choke, and slow regeneration; the Emperor
(55) on his throne up the guards' turbolift, with 22000 health, light
armour, an aura and a proc of Force lightning, a dodge, slow
regeneration, and a last stand. Boba Fett (45) on the far gantry,
waiting for Solo, is a miniboss: 6500 health, a dodge, crits, and a
wrist rocket. Named carriers: TK-421 (18, not at his post in the bay
control room; his E-11), Admiral Motti (26, hoarse, in the conference
room; his cap), Grand Moff Tarkin (35, weak but armoured, on the
overbridge; his code cylinder), the Royal Guard captain (38; a force
pike), Boba Fett (his helmet), Vader (his lightsaber and his helmet),
and the Emperor (his robe). The dianoga (26) in the compactor and
the IT-O interrogation droid (28) are tough single mobs.

Levels, roughly in walking order: stormtroopers 15, squad leaders 16,
detention guards 17, TK-421 18, technicians 19, TIE pilots 20, the scout
trooper 22 (wanders), officers 24, Admiral Motti 26, the dianoga 26, the
interrogation droid 28, death troopers 30, gunners 32, Tarkin 35, Royal
Guards 38, Boba Fett 45, Vader 55, the Emperor 55. Stormtroopers really are bad shots: they carry a permanent
blind effect, so a share of their shots go wide (30 percent for the
rank and file, 25 for squad leaders, 20 for the detention guards). They
are armoured above their level and carry armour plates for Han.

Uniques: TK-421's E-11 blaster rifle (18; crits, Dexterity), Admiral
Motti's cap (26; Intelligence, Charisma, less damage taken), Princess
Leia's sporting blaster (28, by trade; crits, Dexterity, Charisma), Tarkin's
code cylinder (35, held; Intelligence, Charisma, steady), the Royal
Guard force pike (38; a shock proc, Strength, dodge), Darth Vader's
lightsaber (55; red, crits at the cap and hard, Strength, a
little block), Darth Vader's helmet (55; Constitution, Strength, less
damage taken, steady), Boba Fett's helmet (45; sees the hidden, less
damage taken, Dexterity, Constitution), the Emperor's robe (55;
Intelligence, Wisdom, less damage taken, a dodge, sees the invisible). Common finds: stormtrooper helmets and
chestplates, blaster carbines, DH-17 pistols, TIE flight helmets and
suits, a scout's EC-17, officers' tunics, a durasteel pipe in the
compactor, death trooper helmets and DLT-19D heavy blasters, gunners'
helmets, Royal Guard robes. Rolled only: a vibro-scalpel (28), gunner's
gauntlets (33), an armoured greatcoat (35), a Royal Guard's helmet (37), an EE-3 carbine rifle (45),
a black durasteel cuirass (55), a heavy vibro-axe (54).

The freighter (hold 2301 and lounge 2302) is safe. The maintenance
shafts (2312 under the cells; 2318 and 2319 down to the tractor beam)
are dark; Han sells glow rods, and one lies on a rack in the TIE hangar.

Merchants (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- Princess Leia (2323, cell 2187, 2309): her sporting blaster for the
  needle of the IT-O interrogation droid that questioned her (2334,
  which the droid carries).
- R2-D2 (2303, bay control room 2303): an Imperial access card for 50
  silver.
- Han Solo (2300, freighter hold 2301): glow rod 25, medpac 30,
  smuggler's armoured vest 320, DL-44 heavy blaster pistol 450; a
  patched stormtrooper cuirass for 300 silver plus three stormtrooper
  armour plates; an A280 blaster rifle for 450 silver plus two Imperial
  access cards and an armour plate; a glow rod for one armour plate.

Armour plates come from every stormtrooper and TK-421; access cards from
technicians, TIE pilots, officers, and gunners.

Trainers: the ghost of Ben Kenobi (2302, freighter lounge 2302) teaches
dodge and riposte; Luke Skywalker (2322, in borrowed stormtrooper armour
in the bay control room 2303) teaches rescue. Chewbacca (2301) is aboard
the freighter too, and teaches bash. R2-D2 and C-3PO are in the bay control room:
R2-D2 sells Imperial access cards (50), and C-3PO, unarmed, can be
fought for the silver he carries; mouse droids wander
the bay and corridors.

Quest item: 2327, the Death Star plans (planted by questmasters only).
