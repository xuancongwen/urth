# icewind

Icewind Dale in the years of the Crystal Shard: Bryn Shander, the lake
town of Lonelywood, the tundra between, and Clan Battlehammer's mines
under Kelvin's Cairn. Levels 3 to 10. The hub's north door (room 6 in
`start`) opens into a root cellar under Bryn Shander's wall (600). The
mine's deepest room, the Bottom of the Shaft (640), goes down to room
700 in the Underdark.

The towns, Drizzt's cave, Bruenor's hall, and the forge are safe. The
mine tunnels below the gate and Biggrin's lair are dark.
