# winterfell

Winterfell in the last winter before the Long Night returns: the winter
town, the double walls and their gates, the great yard, Mikken's forge,
the kennels, the Great Keep's hall and the lord's solar, the hot-spring
glass gardens, the godswood and its heart tree, the crypts of the Kings
of Winter, and the Wolfswood east of town, where wildlings who came over
the Wall are camped and something worse has followed them. Levels 6 to
14. Reached through the hub's north door (the frost-grey door of the Hall
of Far Doors, room 7 in `start`), which opens in the back of a waystone
on the Kingsroad (1500).

Bosses: the Stone King (14) in the Oldest Tomb under the crypts, the
wildling warlord (11) in the camp, the great direwolf (11) in its den,
the White Walker (14) in the Clearing of the Pale Light beyond the
wights, and past it the Night King (14) at the Heart of the Cold
(1530), the last fight in the area.

The Wolfswood and the crypts are hostile: grey wolves, the great
direwolf, wildling raiders, wights, the White Walker, and the crypt
spiders attack on sight. The shadowcat by the frozen stream lies
hidden, and is on you before you see it.

The Starks and their household. Those with a trade stay peaceful: Ser
Rodrik Cassel, the master-at-arms (13), and Jon Snow (12, with Ghost at
his side) in the great yard, and Maester Luwin (10) in the solar. The
rest fight back if attacked and can be killed for what they carry:
Lord Eddard Stark (14) in the great hall, for Ice, with Lady Catelyn
(10) at his right hand, in wool-lined gloves; Robb (12) in the great
yard, with a castle-forged longsword and a wolf-buckled belt; Theon
Greyjoy (11) on the battlements, with his yew longbow; Arya (8) between
the walls, wanting Needle back, with a filched skinning knife; Rickon
(6) and Shaggydog in the kennels' bent-barred pen; Sansa (8) in the
glass gardens, with a blue winter rose; Bran (9) and Hodor (12) on the
godswood path, Hodor with a sentinel-pine staff; and Old Nan (6) in
the solar, for her knitting needle. Each carries some silver.

Safe: the waystone, the Smoking Log, the forge, and the heart tree. The
great hall is not: Lord Eddard Stark (14) can be killed for Ice,
and Old Nan (6), in the solar, for her knitting needle. Dark: the four
crypt rooms (a torch lies in the First Keep, and the alewife and Mikken
sell more). The iron crypt door starts
closed.

Merchants: Mikken (forge, 1510) sells guard-grade steel for coin, and
makes a wolf-pelt cloak for 180 silver and two grey wolf pelts (wolves
and the direwolf in the Wolfswood carry them) and a dragonglass dagger
for 260 silver and two shards of dragonglass (wildling raiders and the
warlord carry one each, and one lies in the deep crypts). The alewife
(Smoking Log, 1503) sells ale and torches. Maester Luwin in the solar
is a questmaster; the red weirwood leaf (1517) is a quest item.

Trainers: Ser Rodrik Cassel (the yard, 1509) teaches enhanced damage;
Jon Snow (the yard) teaches riposte; Osha (1531, at the heart tree)
teaches the Neutral god's Entangle, Barkskin, and Call Lightning to its
apostles.

The heart tree (1521) is the Neutral god's temple, the only one in the
connected world. Its sacrifice is a weirwood seed (1538), lying under a
young wild weirwood at the frozen stream (1527), where the shadowcat
hunts; Osha's look and the stream's description point to it.

Uniques: the great direwolf carries Needle (1525); the wildling spearwife
carries Longclaw (1524); the wildling warlord carries the
Horn of Joramun; the White Walker wears its shifting mail; the Night
King wears his crown of ice (Constitution, and a cold that bites
back); Theon Greyjoy carries his yew longbow (crits, Dexterity). The
dragonglass dagger now burns what it cuts. Ordinary gear: gloves
(1504), a sword-belt (1506), roundshields on the gatehouse guards, a
sentinel-pine staff on Hodor, a skinning knife (1523), an iron torc in the wolf den (1525), a
stone maul (1527), Night's Watch boots (1528), fur leggings on the
spearwife, and a bronze arm-ring on the warlord.
