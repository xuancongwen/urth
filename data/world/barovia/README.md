# barovia

Barovia, the valley in the mists, and Castle Ravenloft on its pillar of
rock above it: the village of Barovia with its tavern, its church, and
its besieged burgomaster's mansion; the Svalich Woods and their wolves;
the Vistani camp at Tser Pool and the bandits above the falls; the
werewolf den in the deep woods; and the castle itself, from the
drawbridge to the catacombs to the top of the high tower. Levels 12 to
45. Reached south from hub 9 in `start`, through the mists on the Old
Svalich Road (2100).

Levels, roughly in walking order: dire wolves 12 and great dire wolves
14 (assist, wander), zombies 15 at the mansion and in the graveyard,
Strahd zombies 17 in the castle courtyard, Vistani bandits 18 and
Arrigal 20 above Tser Falls, werewolves 22 (wander, regenerate) and
Kiril Stoyanovich 24 in the deep woods, Baba Lysaga 26 in her creeping
hut east of them, vampire spawn 28 (leech) all through the castle, Rahadin 32, the three brides 34, and Strahd von
Zarovich 45.

Boss: Strahd von Zarovich (45) at the top of the high tower (2126):
14000 health, two swings a round, and he leeches and regenerates.
Named carriers: Ireena Kolyana (18, in the burgomaster's mansion; she
is not hostile, but she fights back, and carries a silvered longsword
and silver), Arrigal (20, the Sunsword), Baba Lysaga (26, 950 health,
an aura of biting flies; her bone staff and a flyblown shawl), Rahadin
(32, his scimitar), Ludmilla Vilisevic (34, the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind), Strahd (45, his
cloak, the Tome of Strahd, and a scroll of unholy word, 2132, the Evil
god's found spell; Lady Fiona Wachter's look points to it). Kiril Stoyanovich (24), Anastrasya
Karelova and Volenta Popofsky (34) are named but carry nothing unique.

Uniques: the Sunsword (20; flares with sunlight, sees the invisible,
Strength), Rahadin's scimitar (32; crits, finishes the
wounded, Dexterity), the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind (34, held; a holy
aura, wards, Wisdom), the Tome of Strahd (45, held;
Intelligence and Wisdom, sees the hidden), Strahd's cloak (45; leech,
regeneration, dodge, Dexterity), Baba Lysaga's bone staff
(26, two-handed; blasts of flies, Intelligence and Wisdom), and
Ezmerelda's silvered rapier (24; finesse, crits, Dexterity). Common
finds: grey wolf pelts, grave dirt, Vistani curved knives, rotting livery tabards on the
Strahd zombies, silver bridal circlets on the brides, a lantern in the
catacombs; and from kills, hunters' jerkins, gravediggers' mattocks,
Vistani riding boots, werewolf-hide leggings, boar spears, guards'
halberds, velvet doublets, gilded breastplates, von Zarovich
greatswords and warplate, and a flyblown hag's shawl.

The Blood of the Vine tavern (2104), the church (2107), and Madam Eva's
tent (2113) are safe. The castle catacombs and crypts (2127, 2128,
2129) are dark; Bildrath sells hooded lanterns, and one lies in the
catacombs. The graveyard (2110) has a sealed crypt stair whose `down`
exit is kept free for the Deadite Crypt.

Trainers: Father Donavich (2102, the church 2107) teaches fast healing
and rescue, and to the Good god's apostles Smite and Prayer of Healing; Rudolph van Richten (2120, disguised as Rictavio on the dry
fountain in the village square 2103) teaches coup de grace. Ismark
Kolyanovich (2101) drinks in the tavern and teaches rescue. Lady Fiona
Wachter (2124, peaceful, at the crypt stair in the graveyard 2110)
teaches the Evil god's Dread, Malediction, and Harm to its apostles. Madam Eva
(2103) tells fortunes at Tser Pool and is a questmaster (`quest
request`). The Vistani fiddler is peaceful.

Quest item: 2113, a letter from Kolyan Indirovich (planted by
questmasters only).
