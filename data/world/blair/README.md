# blair

Montgomery Blair High School on University Boulevard, home of the
Blazers and of the Science, Mathematics, and Computer Science Magnet,
as it was in its new building around 2000 to 2004: the bus loop by Four
Corners, the lobby and its trophy cases, the main hallway, the
teachers' workroom and its copier, the cafeteria, the loading dock,
the stadium, the magnet wing with its physics, chemistry, and computer
labs, the Silver Chips newsroom, and the steam tunnels down to the
Roach King. Levels 5 to 9, with the Roach King at 10: an early
levelling area, crowded rooms, a two-minute reset, and loot that is
mostly what the drop rolls give. Reached by the magnet bus, east from
the `tpms` bus loop (4800) to Blair's (4900).

Nobody who works or studies here can be hurt: the security guard, the
magnet coordinator, the teachers, the Silver Chips editor, the
cafeteria worker, and the head custodian are all `peaceful`.
Everything to fight is vermin or a school thing come alive.

Rising from the entrance: German cockroaches (5) in the hallway and
cafeteria; stink bug swarms (5) in the hallway and magnet wing; escaped
lab rats (6) in the magnet wing and chemistry lab; jammed laser
printers (6) in the computer lab; feral vending machines (7) and
mystery meat (7) in the cafeteria; Van de Graaff generators (7) in the
physics lab; fume-hood spills (7) in the chemistry lab; dumpster
raccoons (7) on the loading dock; stadium geese (8); steam-tunnel rats
(8) and American cockroaches (8) in the tunnels; royal roaches (9) in
the court. Printers, mystery meat, spills, raccoons, geese, tunnel
rats, American roaches, and royal roaches attack on sight.

Boss: the Roach King (10, `boss`, 4922), in his court (4913). Named:
the Workroom Copier (8, the teachers' workroom 4903).

Uniques, from loot tables: a red stapler (the copier, one time in four;
mace, blunt, a volley-of-staples proc, Strength) and a TI-89 Titanium
(the Roach King, one time in five; held, Intelligence, Wisdom, crits).

Common finds: nothing is carried. Every kill may roll one piece of
plain drop gear, two at each level from 5 to 10: drafting compass and
Blazers hoodie (5); tennis racket and Converse high-tops (6); pipe
wrench and chemistry lab apron (7); aluminum bat and football helmet
(8); fire extinguisher and catcher's chest protector (9); custodian's
sledgehammer and football shoulder pads (10).
