# xenogears

The world of Xenogears, from Lahan to the throne of Deus: the farming
village of Lahan on the eve of Alice and Timothy's wedding and the night
it burned, Dr. Citan Uzuki's house on the hill, the Black Moon Forest
where Elly sits by her fire, Dazil and the Aveh desert, Bledavik and
Fatima Castle under the usurper Shakhan and the Gebler commander
Vanderkaum, the old waterway under the city where Bart creeps in to
rescue Margie, the sand ship Yggdrasil, the holy city of Nisan, Kislev's
capital Nortune with its Battling arena and D-Block, the ship-city
Thames and the Ethos chapel with its excavation, Shevat floating in the
sky, Solaris and Etrenank (the Elements, Ramsus, the Gazel Ministry,
Miang, Emperor Cain), the buried ruins of Zeboim, the Tower of Babel
where Grahf waits, Weltall-Id and Id, and the Merkaba, with Krelian
in his laboratory and Deus on its throne. Levels 25 to 60. Reached west
from the Hall of Far Futures (room 11 in `start`), through a door scored
by enormous claws that opens in the trunk of a pine beside a crashed
gear outside Lahan (3800).

Raid boss: Deus (60, 45000 health) is meant for a full group of
seven or eight, not for one hero. Its own effects are only steadiness; its
power is in its regalia: a wave of light from the Zohar washes over
everyone fighting it, a surge of the Zohar strikes hard, leech heals
it, the bracer of the Animus slows those it grips, thorns lash whoever
strikes its arms, and fourteen pieces turn most blows. In a group
simulation of level-60 fighters in standard kit (its two guarding
coils left out; retuned 2026-10-12), seven won about half the time
and eight always; a lone fighter lasts about six rounds and barely
marks it.

Attack on sight: the Heimdal gears burning Lahan, the Black Moon
wolves, the sand stalkers and patrol gears of the desert, D-Block's
convicts and the chimeras in the sewers below it, the excavated gears
and Stier in the Ethos dig, the Solarian Gebler soldiers and the
imperial guard of Etrenank, Grahf, Weltall-Id, Id, and the angels and
coils of the Merkaba. Of the bosses, Stier, Grahf, Weltall-Id and Id
start the fight themselves; the rest fight back if attacked but never
start it.

Common finds: pilots' flight jackets and Aveh sabres from the Heimdal
gears at Lahan, patrol rifles from the desert gears, royal guards'
halberds and spired helms, Battling pilots' gauntlets, D-Block shivs
and convicts' padded coats, Ethos relic-hammers from the excavated
gears, Gebler rifles, helmets and officers' greatcoats, Solarian palace
guards' halberds, Merkaba angels' wing-blades and carapaces; and as
rolled loot only, a Zeboim alloy blade (50), a Solarian imperial
cuirass (52), crimson gear-plate gauntlets (55), and a plate of Deus's
hull (60). A Shevat air-guard's spear (42) is sold, not found.

Salvaged gear parts (3800, a `material` item, pool `gear part`) come
out of the Heimdal gears, the desert patrol gears, the Battling
contenders and the excavated gears, about half the time.
