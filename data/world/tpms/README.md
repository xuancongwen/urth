# tpms

Takoma Park Middle School, the math, science, and computer science
magnet on Piney Branch Road, as it was around 1997 to 2000: the bus
loop under the NUCLEAR FREE ZONE sign, the front office and its drawer
of confiscated Tamagotchis, the magnet math room, the fruit-fly lab,
the computer lab with Oregon Trail and a Logo turtle, rectangle-pizza
Friday in the cafeteria, the kitchen and the boiler room, the gym and
its PACER test, the field, and the Sligo Creek trail in a Brood X
summer. Levels 1 to 5, with the Goose of Sligo Creek at 6: an early
levelling area, crowded rooms, an eight-minute reset, and loot that is
mostly what the drop rolls give. Reached down the bus steps in the
floor of the Hall of Flickering Windows (room 13) to the bus loop
(4800); the magnet bus runs on east from there to `blair`.

Nobody who works or studies here can be hurt: the crossing guard, the
secretary, the teachers, the lunch lady, and the custodian are all
`peaceful`. Everything to fight is vermin or a school thing come
alive.

Rising from the entrance: cockroaches (1) in the hallway, cafeteria,
and kitchen; stink bugs (1) on the math room sills and the magnet
hallway; fruit flies (2) in the science lab; runaway Logo turtles (2)
in the computer lab; kitchen rats (3) in the kitchen and boiler room;
feral dodgeballs (3) in the gym; Brood X cicadas (3) on the field and
the trail; flying boiler-room roaches (4); Canada geese (4) on the
field, the trail, and under the bridge; snapping turtles (5) on the
trail and under the bridge. Fruit flies, dodgeballs, flying roaches,
geese, and turtles attack on sight.

Boss: the Goose of Sligo Creek (6, `boss`, 4817), under the footbridge
(4813). Named: the PACER test boom box (5, the gym 4809).

Uniques, from loot tables: a pair of light-up sneakers (the boom box,
one time in four; feet, Dexterity, dodge) and the Sacred Ticonderoga
(the goose, one time in five; dagger, pierce, an erasure proc,
Intelligence).

Common finds: nothing is carried. Every kill may roll one piece of
plain drop gear, two at each level from 1 to 6: twelve-inch ruler and
hooded sweatshirt (1); meter stick and JanSport backpack (2); push
broom and lunch tray (3); field hockey stick and safety goggles (4);
lacrosse stick and gym shorts (5); drafting T-square and puffy winter
parka (6).
