# zelda

Hyrule, from The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, with nods to the
wider series. Kokiri Forest and the Great Deku Tree with Queen Gohma in
its roots; Hyrule Field, where Stalchildren crawl out at night and
Peahats whirl, and Lon Lon Ranch; Castle Town with the Bazaar and the
Potion Shop, the Temple of Time with the Master Sword in its pedestal,
and the castle courtyard where Princess Zelda watches through a window;
Kakariko Village, its graveyard, and the Shadow Temple beneath it;
Death Mountain, Goron City, Dodongo's Cavern and King Dodongo, and the
Great Fairy's fountain at the summit; Zora's River, Zora's Domain, and
Barinade in Lord Jabu-Jabu's belly; the Lost Woods, the Sacred Forest
Meadow, and Phantom Ganon in the Forest Temple; Lake Hylia, the Water
Temple, Dark Link and Morpha; Gerudo Valley, the Gerudo fortress, the
Desert Colossus, and Twinrova in the Spirit Temple; and last Ganon's
Castle, where Ganondorf plays his organ at the top of the tower. Levels
5 to 35. Reached through the Triforce-carved door north of the Hall of
Old Tales (room 12 in `start`), which opens into Kokiri Forest (3900).

Levels in walking order: Deku Babas 5, Mido 6, Skulltulas 7,
Stalchildren 8, Talon 8, Queen Gohma 9, Peahats 10, Poes 11, Ingo 11,
Tektites 12, Dodongos 13, Lizalfos 14, Octoroks 14, King Dodongo 15,
Biri 15, Princess Ruto 15, Barinade 17, Skull Kids 17, Wolfos 18,
Moblins 18, Stalfos 20, Darunia 20, Phantom Ganon 21, river Zoras 21,
Like Likes 23, Dark Link 24, ReDeads 24, Morpha 25, the Dead Hand 26,
Gerudo guards 27, Bongo Bongo 28, Leevers 28, Nabooru 29, Iron Knuckles
30, Sheik 30, Twinrova 31, Stalfos knights 33, Ganondorf 35. The Shadow
Temple (24 to 28) is under the Kakariko graveyard (11), behind a shut
tomb door: it is the one place where the levels jump, and the door is
the warning.

Bosses, each the last fight of its stretch and meant for a group: a
lone fighter of its level loses in five to eight rounds, having dealt a
fifth to a quarter of its health. Queen Gohma (9, 360 health; the eye
of Queen Gohma, and the Kokiri Emerald inside her), King Dodongo (15,
750; the Goron's Bracelet, and the Goron's Ruby), Barinade (17, 900;
the Boomerang, and Zora's Sapphire), Phantom Ganon (21, 950; his spear
and the Forest Medallion), Morpha (25, 1500; the Water Medallion),
Bongo Bongo (28, 2650; the Hover Boots and the Shadow Medallion),
Twinrova (31, 3600; the Mirror Shield, the Spirit Medallion, and the
key to Ganon's Castle), and Ganondorf (35, 6000, flagged `boss`; his
sword, the Triforce of Power, and the Gerudo King's mantle), the final
fight: a lone level-35 fighter lasts about five rounds and takes a
fifth of his health. Beaten, they say, he becomes Ganon; Ganon is not
built. Minibosses: Dark Link (24, 1200; his sword, and he mirrors the
blows struck at him) in the Room of Mist, and the Dead Hand (26, 2000;
the Lens of Truth) in the Shadow Temple.

Famous people who fight back if attacked but never start it, and can be
killed for what they carry: Mido (6, the Kokiri Sword and a Deku
shield), Talon (8, asleep in the stables; a pitchfork, a bottle of milk,
and his purse), Ingo (11; his riding crop), Princess Ruto (15, in
Jabu-Jabu's belly; her silver scale), Darunia (20, in his chamber; the
Megaton Hammer), Nabooru (29, at the Desert Colossus; the Silver
Gauntlets), and Sheik (30, in the Temple of Time; Sheik's needles and
the Triforce of Wisdom). Darunia, Nabooru and Sheik are strong: a lone
fighter of their level loses.

Common finds: Deku sticks and shields, Kokiri tunics, stalchild femurs,
travellers' caps, a farmer's pitchfork, Hylian broadswords, grave
shrouds, Goron stone bracers, Lizalfos blades, Zora-scale mail, Skull
Kid masks, Moblin spears, Wolfos-pelt cloaks, broken Giant's Knives and
Stalfos round shields, river Zora greaves, half-digested Hylian helms,
ReDead wrappings, Sheikah knives and iron masks, Gerudo scimitars,
veils and silk trousers, Iron Knuckle axes, helms and breastplates,
Stalfos knights' greatswords, dark knights' gauntlets, and black
cuirasses of Ganon's guard. Several of these are rolled loot only
(`drop`).

Kokiri Forest (3900) and its shop (3901), Castle Town (the market
3913, the Bazaar 3914, the Potion Shop 3915), the castle courtyard
(3917), Kakariko Village (3920), Goron City (3925), Zora's Domain
(3932), and the Great Fairy's fountain (3930) are safe. Inside the
Deku Tree (3904, 3905), the Forest Temple (3908, 3909), Dodongo's
Cavern (3927, 3928) and the Shadow Temple (3922, 3923) are dark: the
Kokiri shop and the Bazaar sell bottled fairies, Dampé sells lanterns,
and a bottled fairy lies in the Deku Tree's meadow (3903). The Sheikah
tomb door (between 3921 and 3922) starts shut. The gate of Ganon's
Castle (between 3917 and 3918) is locked and cannot be picked: its key,
Ganon's boss key (3965), is carried by Twinrova.
