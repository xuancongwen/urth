# cadia

The Fall of Cadia, in the grim darkness of the far future: a Cadian
trench line facing the Eye of Terror, the cratered Elysion Fields
beyond the wire, the broken fortress-city of Kasr Myrak with its
catacombs, an Ork fort dug into a manufactorum, the Black Legion in the
defiled cathedral and at the foot of a cracked pylon, a field of skulls
where a Greater Daemon of Khorne sits a brass throne, and at the fallen
Cadian Gate, the breach that faces the Eye, Abaddon the Despoiler.
Levels 22 to 65, the highest area in the world. Reached west from the
lowest landing of the stair below the Deeper Hall (room 10 in `start`),
which opens onto the trench mouth (1800).

Bosses: Nob Gutrippa (36) in the big gun pit; Zaraphiston the sorcerer
(46) on the pylon's ritual gallery, with the Staff of Changing; the Bloodthirster (55) at the brass
throne; Haarken Worldclaimer (60), the Herald of the Apocalypse, on the
rubble of the fallen Gate among the Terminators; Abaddon the Despoiler
(65) at the Eye-facing breach. The last three are raid bosses for a
full group, one after another: the Bloodthirster 165000 health,
Haarken 290000, Abaddon 390000. In a group simulation of fighters of
each one's level in standard kit (2026-10-12), seven won about half
the time and eight always; a lone fighter lasts five or six rounds. Named
carriers: the cult demagogue (24, a bolt pistol) on the Valkyrie wreck,
and Lieutenant Dravik (28, a Cadian chainsword, and the rank pins the
Lord Castellan wants) in the ossuary under the kasr.

Beyond the wire the enemy attacks on sight: Chaos cultists, traitor
guardsmen, plague zombies, Ork boyz, shoota boyz and 'ard boyz, the
Chaos Space Marines, the bloodletters, and the Bloodthirster itself.
The Black Legion marines stand watch and see hiding and sneaking
intruders. The demagogue, Dravik, the Terminators, and the other bosses
wait to be challenged.

Uniques: the bolt pistol (bolt detonations, crits, Dexterity), the
Cadian chainsword (chain-teeth procs, crits, Strength), the power fist
(Gutrippa; worn on the hands, bare hands hit harder, power attacks,
Strength), the shard of the Cadian pylon (Zaraphiston; held, steadies
and protects, sees the invisible, Wisdom and Intelligence), the Staff of Changing
(1835, 46, Zaraphiston's; arcane spells stronger, harder to resist,
and surging for 1.75x, Intelligence), the axe of
the Bloodthirster (leech, crits, burns, Strength), the Helspear (Haarken; procs when hurled, crits, Strength and
Dexterity), the Talon of Horus (Abaddon; crits, hard crits, combi-bolter procs, leech, Strength and
Constitution). By trade: Duty, Lord Castellan Creed's laspistol (30;
crits, hot-shot procs, Dexterity), a spirit stone of Ulthwé
(46; neck, a dodge, sees the invisible, Intelligence and Wisdom), a
feather of the Living Saint (50; neck, regeneration, less damage taken,
Wisdom and Constitution). Common finds: cultist autoguns, rotted greatcoats, Ork
choppas and 'eavy armour, Black Legion boltguns and horned helms,
hellblades, brass greaves, Terminator pauldrons, reaper autocannons
and Terminator plate (all rolled loot), lumens in the wreck and the
bunker, and a promethium drum in the silent battery.

The trench (1800, 1801, the dugout 1802, the Commissariat bunker
1803, the firestep 1804, the field chapel 1805) is safe, and Cadian
Shock Troopers (25) stand in it, peaceful; a servo-skull wanders the
line. The collapsed bunker, the catacombs and the ossuary (1813 to
1815) are dark; the quartermaster sells lumens, and two lie in the
area.

Lord Castellan Ursarkar Creed (1820) and Colour Sergeant Jarran Kell
(1821) stand at a map in the fire trench (1801). Saint Celestine (1822)
stands on the rubble at the breach of Kasr Myrak (1811), and Eldrad
Ulthran (1823), unseen by the traitors, at the foot of the pylon
(1822). All four are peaceful.

Trainers: Commissar Raskel (1801, Commissariat bunker 1803) teaches
berserk and rescue; Colour Sergeant Kell (1821, fire trench 1801)
teaches second wind and enhanced damage; Eldrad Ulthran (1823, room
1822) teaches arcane spells to those who have the school: insight,
confusion, repulsion, globe of invulnerability, true seeing, hold
monster, and mislead.

Scroll (`study`, a found spell no trainer sells): Zaraphiston (1813,
at the pylon 1823) carries a scroll of the finger of death (1833);
Eldrad's look hints at it.

Merchants (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- Quartermaster Venn (1800, Munitorum dugout 1802): lumen 30 (or one
  promethium), Cadian helmet 400, Cadian flak armour 550, M36 lasgun
  600; Kasrkin carapace armour for 800 silver plus three promethium,
  Kasrkin hellgun for 900 silver plus two promethium.
- Lord Castellan Creed (1820, fire trench 1801): Duty, his hot-shot
  laspistol, for Lieutenant Dravik's rank pins (1829, from Dravik in
  the ossuary).
- Saint Celestine (1822, breach of Kasr Myrak 1811): a feather of the
  Living Saint for four vials of daemon ichor.
- Eldrad Ulthran (1823, foot of the pylon 1822): a spirit stone of
  Ulthwé for the shard of the Cadian pylon (Zaraphiston's, just above);
  a wraithbone staff (1834; 50, Intelligence, arcane spells stronger
  and surging, mana) for 8000 silver.
- Confessor Hallam (1803, field chapel 1805): purity seal 400; blessed
  eviscerator for 1500 silver plus three vials of daemon ichor.

Promethium comes from traitor guardsmen, the demagogue, Ork boyz and
shoota boyz, Gutrippa, and the silent battery; daemon ichor from the
bloodletters, Zaraphiston, and the Bloodthirster.

Quest item: 1806, the standard of the Cadian 8th (planted by
questmasters only).
