# arrakis

Arrakis, the desert planet, Dune: Sietch Tabr in the Shield Wall, the
old caves behind it where Fremen renegades hide, the broken land and the
Funeral Plain beyond the door seal, spice blows worked by sandtrout and
Harkonnen spice-guards, the wreck of a spice harvester, a Harkonnen
picket line and the forward base behind it where the Sardaukar,
Reverend Mother Mohiam and Feyd-Rautha wait, and the deep desert where
Shai-Hulud swims. Levels 14 to 60. Reached east from hub room 10, a
crack in the rock that opens onto the Shield Wall (1900).

Bosses: Shai-Hulud (60, the roaming world boss of the deep desert,
90000 health, soaks blows, a storm of sand around it, flagged wander),
Feyd-Rautha (44, two swings a round, poisoned cuts), Reverend Mother
Mohiam (42, the Voice), the Sardaukar Bashar (38), Captain Iakin Nefud
(32). Named carriers: Staban Tuek the smuggler (26, the thumper), the
renegade naib (28, the crysknife), Captain Nefud (the Fremen
stillsuit), the Bashar (the Sardaukar blade), Mohiam (the Gom Jabbar),
Feyd-Rautha (his poisoned blade).

Uniques: the crysknife of a maker's tooth (28; crits, finesse,
Dexterity), a Fremen stillsuit (32; regeneration, Constitution), a
thumper (26; a thumping proc, Strength), the Sardaukar blade (38; crits,
weapon damage, steadiness), the Gom Jabbar (42, held; executes the
weak, steadiness, Wisdom), Feyd-Rautha's poisoned blade (44; envenoms,
crits, dodge). Common finds: scorpion stingers, sandtrout-skin gloves,
Harkonnen kindjals, cuirasses, helmets and lasguns, Holtzman shield
belts and maula pistols from the renegades, Nefud's officer's cuirass,
Sardaukar combat harness, and as rolled loot only, Feyd's arena
vambraces (43), a sandworm's tooth (60) and worm-ring hide (60).

Sietch Tabr (1900 to 1905) is safe; Stilgar and his Fremen are
peaceful. A closed door seal (1905) keeps the sand and the worm out;
the blast door (1917) shuts the forward base. The old caves (1910 to
1913) are dark: Farok sells glowglobes, and one lies in the passage.
Watch out: the caves go from bats (15) straight to renegades (28).

Merchant (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- Farok the water-seller (1900, alcove 1903): glowglobe 30, literjon of
  water 10, Fremkit 60, fixed crysknife 200; sietch-made stillsuit for
  150 silver plus two measures of spice; maker hooks for 250 plus four
  spice; a glowglobe or a literjon for one spice.

Trainer: Chani (1901, training floor 1904) teaches feint and backstab.

Spice (1900, a `material` item, pool `spice`) lies at the three blows
(1914, 1918, 1926) and is carried by sandtrout, Harkonnens, renegades,
Staban Tuek and the worm.

Quest item: 1926, a string of water rings (planted by questmasters only).
