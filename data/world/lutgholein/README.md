# lutgholein

Lut Gholein, the jewel of the desert on the shore of the Twin Seas, and
the desert of Aranoch around it: the sewers below the city, the Rocky
Waste and the Dry Hills with the Halls of the Dead, the Far Oasis and
the Maggot Lair, the Lost City and the Claw Viper Temple, the palace
harem and cellar, the Arcane Sanctuary, and the Canyon of the Magi with
Tal Rasha's Tomb at its heart. Levels 28 to 38. Reached by Warriv's
caravan, east from the Rogue Encampment (`rogue`, room 3101) to the
west gate (3200); Meshif's ship at the docks (3201) sails east for
Kurast (`kurast`, room 3300). Every room of the city is safe. The
sewers, the tombs, the lair, the temple, the palace's inner rooms, the
Sanctuary, and Tal Rasha's Tomb are dark; Elzix sells desert lamps, and
one lies at the foot of the sewer ladder.

Levels, roughly in walking order: sand raiders and horrors 28 (the
sewers are under the harbour road), Geglash 29, sabre cats 29,
Radament the Fallen 30 (miniboss; the Book of Skill), hollow ones 30
(embalming linen), Bloodwitch the Wild 31 (miniboss; the Horadric Cube), dung
soldiers 31 (scarab chitin, sparks back when struck), rock worms 32,
Beetleburst 32 (miniboss), Coldworm the Burrower 33 (miniboss; lays
rock worms as she fights; the Staff of Kings), invaders 33, claw vipers
34 (poison), specters 34 (chill), Fangskin 35 (miniboss; the Amulet of
the Viper), Fire Eye 35 (miniboss; Hellplague), the Summoner 36
(miniboss; Ondal's Wisdom), unravelers 36, tomb guardians 37, Ancient
Kaa the Soulless 37 (miniboss; his wrappings), and Duriel, Lord of
Pain, 38 (the final boss, 5000 health, two swings a round, a chilling
grip, and a holy freeze aura; carries Duriel's Shell and Tal Rasha's
Lidless Eye). Tyrael hangs chained in Duriel's chamber, beyond help.

Minibosses' health: Radament 2400, Bloodwitch 2400, Beetleburst 1800,
Coldworm 3000, Fangskin 3000, Fire Eye 2800, the Summoner 3400, Ancient
Kaa 3400.

Duriel's chamber (3229) is below a locked, pickproof seal in Tal
Rasha's Tomb (3228), the Orifice, whose key is the restored Horadric
Staff (3207). Lysander makes it from the Staff of Kings (Coldworm) and
the Amulet of the Viper (Fangskin), and 500 silver.

Uniques: Radament's Book of Skill (30, held; skill cooldowns a round
shorter, Intelligence and Wisdom), Atma's Scarab (31; crits, punishes
the staggered, Dexterity), the Horadric Cube (a container that holds
150), the Staff of Kings (33; Intelligence and Wisdom), the Amulet of
the Viper (35; sees the hidden and the invisible, Dexterity and
Intelligence), Hellplague (35; a long sword that burns and festers),
the restored Horadric Staff (36; both sights, Intelligence and Wisdom,
wards; the Orifice key), Ondal's Wisdom (36; Intelligence and Wisdom,
soaks, steadies, arcane spells 10 percent stronger, a fifth more mana), Ancient Kaa's wrappings (37; regeneration, soak,
Constitution), Duriel's Shell (38; less damage taken, Constitution and
Strength, frost that bites back), Tal Rasha's Lidless Eye (38, held;
both sights, cooldowns a round shorter, Intelligence and Wisdom). Fara
makes a scarab-shell shield (33; blocks, sparks back). Common finds:
desert scimitars and raiders' head-wraps, sewer guards' hauberks,
sabre-cat boots, bronze khopeshes, chitin vambraces, spiked gauntlets,
viper-scale hauberks, unravelers' glaives, gilded death-masks; and
from kills, Horadric war-blades and suits of Horadric scale.

  spells cheaper and harder to resist) 2000.

- Jerhyn (3205, before the palace gates 3206): palace guard's

Trainers: Greiz (3208, the inn 3209) teaches bash and rescue. Drognan
(3209, among his books 3210) teaches arcane spells to those who have
the school: cone of cold, phantasmal killer, iron body, Tenser's
transformation, cloudkill, globe of invulnerability, true seeing,
hold monster, chain lightning, mislead, circle of death, and
disintegrate.

Scrolls (`study`, found spells no trainer sells): the Summoner (3225,
room 3226) carries a scroll of the weird (3233); a scroll of
prismatic ward (3234) lies on the floor of Tal Rasha's Chamber (3229)
under Duriel. Drognan's look hints at both.

Geglash the drunk (3203, 29) is not peaceful: thrown out of Atma's
tavern, he drinks outside the gate in the Rocky Waste (3214), fights
back if attacked, and carries his mercenary's spear, his old sewer
guard's hauberk, and 120 silver.

Quest item: 3216, a page of Horazon's journal (planted by questmasters
only).
