# ratcellar

The Cellar of Ten Rats: the cellar under Midgaard's Grunting Boar Inn,
where the innkeeper's boy has been sent to deal with the rats and has
instead been waiting for you. There are a good deal more than ten. The
ale cellar, the wine racks, and the root cellar give way, through a
collapsed wall, to the city's old sewers, a flooded cistern, a warren
the rats gnawed for themselves, and the throne of bones where the Rat
King holds court. A farming area: levels 2 to 5, with the Rat King at
6, rooms crowded with rats, and a reset every eight minutes. Reached by
the cellar trapdoor in the floor of the Grunting Boar (3006, `down`),
which opens onto the foot of the cellar stair (2500).

Boss: the Rat King (6), a knot of a dozen rats bound by their tails, on
its throne (2514), with dire rats around it. Named carrier: Mortimer
the wererat (5), in his den off the cistern (2510).

Uniques: the Rat King's crown (head; Constitution), carried
by the Rat King; Mortimer's moonstone ring on a bit of string (finger;
dodge and Dexterity), carried by Mortimer.

Common finds: every kill can roll the area's rusty cellar gear, two or
three plain pieces at each level from 1 to 7 (a paring knife and a wool
cap; a cellarman's mallet, a jerkin, and muddy boots; a rusty short
sword and a pot helm; a cooper's hatchet, barrel-hoop bracers, and
sewerman's waders; a pitchfork and a studded vest; a meat cleaver,
iron gauntlets, and a cask-lid shield; a chain-and-bucket flail and a
rusted chain shirt). Every rat carries a tail and every spider a skein
of silk, for trading. A candle lies on the bottom step, and a hollowed
cheese in the root cellar.

The foot of the stair (2500) is safe and lit. Everything past it is
dark: buy a candle from Pip.

Merchant (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- Pip, the innkeeper's boy (2500, the foot of the stair): tallow candle
  6; a ratcatcher's leather apron for 20 silver and six rat tails; a
  ratcatcher's cudgel for 30 silver and eight rat tails; spider-silk
  gloves for 30 silver and three skeins of spider silk.

Trainer: Old Nettle the ratcatcher (2500) teaches dodge and trip.

Rats: cellar rats (2) in the cellar rooms, sewer rats (3) from the
collapsed wall to the junction, giant rats (4) in the culvert, cistern,
warren, and nest, dire rats (5) in the cistern and past the warren.
Swarms of rats (3) wander, and attack on sight. Giant cellar spiders (4) hold the
web-choked drain. All the rats assist each other.

Quest item: 2503, a dusty bottle of the Boar's best (planted by
questmasters only).
