# diablo

Tristram, the village in Khanduras, and the ruined cathedral whose
cellars go down into Hell. Reached through the south door of the Deeper
Hall (room 8), which opens as a door in a hillock on the north road into
the village (1300). The village is peaceful and mostly safe; everything
from the cathedral stair down is dark, so buy a lantern from Ogden.

Levels, roughly in walking order: fallen ones and zombies 14, burning
dead skeletons 15, the Butcher 16 (boss; his cleaver), the Skeleton King
17 (boss; the Undead Crown), winged fiends 17, goatmen 18 (halberds),
magma demons and horned demons 19 (demon brains, demon hearts),
succubi and advocates 20, blood knights 21 (demon hearts), Archbishop
Lazarus 21 (boss; the mitre), and Diablo 24 (the final boss, 3200 health,
two swings a round and a burning touch; carries the red soulstone).

Merchants (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- Ogden (1302): brass lantern, red healing potion.
- Griswold (1303): broadsword, full helm, gothic breastplate, tower
  shield; reforges the Butcher's cleaver plus 300 silver into the
  Butcher's Edge.
- Pepin (1305): red healing potion; a demon's brain plus 120 silver buys
  the amulet of mending.
- Adria (1307): a black mushroom (grotto, 1319) plus 200 silver for the
  ring of the dark eye; two demon hearts plus 500 silver for the witch's
  staff; Diablo's red soulstone plus two demon hearts for Arkaine's Valor.
- Wirt (1308): a ring of the zodiac, for 1500 silver and no questions.
