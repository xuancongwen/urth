# palanthas

Palanthas, the city of the Great Library, and its Tower of High Sorcery,
seat of the Black Order (necromancy and enchantment; RULES 6.1). Levels
15 to 22. Reached west from Gateway, the crossroads on the Haven Road
(6101), by the Vingaard Road (6300).

The city is safe: the old gate, the Great Square, and Astinus's Great
Library, where an Aesthetic tells what the grove does. North of the
square is the Shoikan Grove, the Tower's only door and its first test:
grasping dead hands (18), a wraith with a grave-frost blade (19),
and at the heart the warden of the grove (21, boss, 900 health, black
iron greaves). The Tower's iron gate is shut, not locked. Everything in
the grove and the Test attacks from level 1.

Inside, safe: the foyer, the library (the keeper of the black books
teaches the Black Order's school spells to its sworn specialists), and
the black robing room (a dark elf seamstress sells the black robe and a
staff of black wood). Up the stair is the Test: your twin (17), the
climbing dead (18), and the shadow of the Dark Queen (20, boss,
a pendant of the dark moon). Past them, the Chamber of the Test (6317,
`order: black`), where Dalamar the Dark (24, peaceful) hears the oath:
`swear necromancy` or `swear enchantment`.

East of the square, the street runs down to the harbour and the ship to
Southern Ergoth and the ruins of Daltigoth (`daltigoth`).
