# foundation

Isaac Asimov's Foundation: Terminus at the rim of the Galaxy, with the
Time Vault where Hari Seldon's recorded image appears at each crisis,
Salvor Hardin's mayoralty, the Encyclopedia offices and the Traders'
market; then a jump at a time east across the dying Empire. Anacreon
and Prince Regent Wienis with his battle cruiser; Korell and Commdor
Asper Argo; ruined Siwenna, where Bel Riose camps and Ducem Barr waits
by his fire; Trantor, with Streeling University (Hari Seldon in life,
Dors Venabili, Raych), the Galactic Library (Ebling Mis) and Star's End
below it, the palace and Eto Demerzel, and a sector already sacked;
Haven and the Darells; and Kalgan, where the Mule sits on his throne.
Levels 20 to 45. Reached north from the Hall of Far Futures (room 11 in
`start`), which opens onto the Terminus spaceport (3600).

Raid boss: the Mule (45, 17000 health) is meant for a full group of
seven, not for one hero. The Visi-Sonor's tide of despair washes over
everyone fighting him, the leech in his regalia heals him as he
strikes, a chill slows those he grips, and fourteen pieces of regalia
turn most blows. In a group simulation against level-45 fighters in
standard gear, six won one fight in ten and seven nearly always; a
lone fighter lasts about eight rounds and barely marks him. Players
in better gear will need fewer. He was Magnifico Giganticus, the
clown of Haven, all along; the clown's corner in the Darells' rooms is
empty.

Bosses and named carriers: Han Pritcher (43, 9000 health, the
converted general before the black door; his blaster and the
coordinates of Star's End), Eto Demerzel (45, 9000, far stronger than
he looks; the robe of the First Minister), Bel Riose (35, 5600; his
sword), Prince Regent Wienis (28, 3000; the Prince Regent's blaster),
Commdor Asper Argo (30, 1800; the Commdora's necklace), Prince Lefkin
(25, 1200, on the cruiser's bridge; a naval greatcoat). Famous people
who fight back if attacked but never start it: Bayta Darell (36, 2400;
her blaster), Hari Seldon (38, 2600; his cane), Ebling Mis (34, 1400,
frail; his spectacles), Arkady Darell (22, 700, stowed away on Kalgan;
her transcriber).

Uniques: the Mule's regalia, all level 45 and all worn by him: the
hood of the First Citizen (head; Intelligence, Wisdom, steady, less
damage taken), the Mule's chain of office (neck; Charisma,
Constitution, less damage taken), the Mule's padded shoulders
(shoulders; Constitution, Strength, soak), the robe of the Union of
Worlds (body, plate; Constitution, less damage taken, regeneration),
the Mule's sleeves of black mesh (arms; Strength, Dexterity, a wash of
dread on those who strike), the Mule's long gloves (hands; Dexterity,
crits, harder crits), the bracer of despair (wrist; a slowing chill,
Wisdom), the bracer of ecstasy (wrist; leech, Charisma), the Mule's
shield belt (belt; block, less damage taken, Constitution), the Mule's
riding breeches (legs; Dexterity, Strength, dodge), Magnifico's
long-toed boots (feet; dodge, Dexterity), the ring of converted
loyalty (finger; last stand, Constitution, Wisdom), the signet of the
First Citizen (finger; executes, Charisma, Intelligence); and above
them all the Visi-Sonor (55, held; a surge of despair on everyone
near, a crashing chord of terror, leech, Charisma, Intelligence,
steady), the best item in the area. Others: the Prince Regent's
blaster (28; crits, Dexterity), the Commdora's necklace (30, neck;
less damage taken, Charisma), Bel Riose's sword (35; crits, steady,
Strength), Bayta Darell's blaster (36; crits, executes, Dexterity),
Ebling Mis's spectacles (34, face; Intelligence, Wisdom, sees the
hidden), Hari Seldon's cane (38, staff; Intelligence, Wisdom, steady,
shorter cooldowns), Han Pritcher's blaster (43; crits, Dexterity,
Wisdom, steady), the robe of the First Minister (45, body;
Constitution, Strength, regeneration, less damage taken), Arkady's
transcriber (22, held; Intelligence, dodge, sees the hidden), and the
Prime Radiant (42, held; Intelligence, Wisdom, shorter cooldowns,
steady), which only the First Speaker gives.

Common finds: Anacreonian pikes and breastplates, royal guards' plumed
helms, naval greatcoats, Korellian police blasters and steel
breastplates, Imperial blaster rifles, legionary armour and officers'
gauntlets, palace guards' halberds and helms, torn conduit and looted
mantles from the scavengers of the Sack, Kalganian vibro-blades and
mercenaries' jerkins, the converted soldiers' blasters and
breastplates, the Mule's guard helms and halberds; and as rolled loot
only, gauntlets of the Mule's guard (44), a Kalganian warlord's cuirass
(45), and a neuronic whip (45).

Terminus (3600 to 3606), the temple on Anacreon (3610), Ducem Barr's
house (3618), Star's End (3634) and the galleries of Haven (3626) are
safe. The sacked sector of Trantor (3625) is dark: glow-tubes are sold
on Terminus and Haven, and one lies at the corridor checkpoint (3620).
The black door to the throne room (between 3631 and 3632) starts
closed.

Merchants (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- Hober Mallow (3606, the Traders' market): atomic glow-tube 30,
  atomic knife 300, personal force shield 400, nuclear blaster 500; an
  atomic transmuter for 900 silver plus two spent power cells; a force
  shield for 200 plus two cells; a blaster for 250 plus three cells; a
  glow-tube for one cell.
- Lewis Pirenne (3602, Encyclopedia offices): glow-tube 30, or one for
  a spent power cell.
- Poly Verisof (3610, the temple on Anacreon): glow-tube 30, or one for
  a spent power cell.
- Toran Darell (3626, Haven): glow-tube 30, atomic knife 320, personal
  force shield 420; a glow-tube for a power cell.
- The First Speaker (3634, Star's End): the Prime Radiant for the
  coordinates of Star's End, which Han Pritcher carries.

Spent atomic power cells (3600, a `material` item, pool `power cell`)
are carried by Anacreonian soldiers and sailors, Lefkin, Korellians,
legionaries, palace guards, scavengers and mercenaries.

Questmaster: Hari Seldon's recorded image (3605, the Time Vault) hands
out quests and sells for quest points: glow-tube 10, atomic knife 25,
force shield 30, nuclear blaster 35, atomic transmuter 80.

Trainers (`practice`): Salvor Hardin (3603) teaches feint and disarm;
Ducem Barr (3618) fast healing and second wind; Dors Venabili (3621)
twin and riposte; Raych (3621) backstab and dirt kicking.

Quest item: 3602, Ebling Mis's notebook (planted by questmasters
only).
