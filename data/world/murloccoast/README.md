# murloccoast

The Murloc Coast: the Westfall shore past Elwynn's last wheat field,
where the dunes run down to a long grey beach and the murlocs have
taken everything else. A lighthouse on the dunes, a tide line and
shell beach, tide pools full of makrura, two clusters of murloc mud
huts round a spawning lagoon, a wrecked cog, a reef in the shallows,
and a sea grotto under the headland where the Murloc King keeps his
court. Levels 8 to 13. This is a farming area: tight levels, crowded
rooms, murlocs that all come when one is hit (`assist`), a two-minute
reset, and plain drop gear at every level from 7 to 14. Reached west
from Azeroth's wheat fields (1411 in `warcraft`) onto the dune path
(2600).

Rising from the entrance: foragers (8) on the dunes, tide line, and
drying racks; tidehunters (9) on Shell Beach; makrura snapclaws (9) in
the tide pools; murloc warriors (10) through the hut village;
coastrunners (10) wandering the shore from the village to the
headland; oracles (11) at the village heart and the spawning pools;
tiderunners (11) in the shallows and on the reef; royal guards (12) in
the grotto.

Boss: King Mrgl-Mrgl (13, 2616, the crown of shells). Named carrier:
Old Murk-Eye (12, the shipwreck 2611, the fish on a stick).

Uniques (minor): the crown of shells (head; Constitution,
dodge) and the fish on a stick (a dried cod on a spar; crits,
Strength). Common finds: murloc fins on every murloc, lumpy pearls on
the oracles, royal guards, and king, makrura claws from the
snapclaws, and a storm lantern hung on the wreck's figurehead. Plain
drop gear, two or three pieces a level: driftwood fishing spear and
oilskin coat (7); barbed fishing spear, sharkskin boots, cork-float
belt (8); gutting knife, sharkskin gloves, crawler-shell buckler (9);
murloc war spear, scale coif, sharkskin leggings (10); coral-headed
club, sharkskin jerkin, scale vambraces (11); fisherman's trident,
scale hauberk, scale greaves (12); whaler's harpoon, reef-plate helm,
scale mantle (13); whaler's flensing axe, reef-plate cuirass (14).

Safe: the lighthouse (2602) and the lamp gallery above it (2603), where
the ghost of Captain Grayson (15) teaches dodge and trip. Dark: the
grotto mouth (2615) and the king's grotto (2616); the fisherman sells
storm lanterns, and one hangs at the wreck.

Merchant (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- Fisherman Haldren (2600, the lighthouse 2602): storm lantern 25,
  barbed fishing spear 160; crawler-shell buckler for 60 silver plus
  two makrura claws; sharkskin jerkin for 120 silver plus four murloc
  fins; scale hauberk for 250 silver plus three murloc fins and a
  lumpy pearl. He buys fins (worth 6), claws, and pearls like any
  merchant, at half their value.

Quest item: 2605, a barnacled brass spyglass (planted by questmasters
only).
