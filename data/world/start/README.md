# start

The Armory: where new characters wake and where the dead come back. No
fights here but the tutorial's cellar rats; every room but the corridor
is safe.

**The tutorial corridor (40 to 46).** New characters wake in room 40
(`world.start_room`). Each room teaches one thing: help, movement, a
locked pine door and its key (42), gear (a practice sword and a padded
vest, 43), a fight (cellar rats, 44, up to three), and talking. The wall
at the end (46) sums it up: recall, simple spells up the stair from the
hall, a god's favour given up on its altar, merchants, and help.

**The armory (1).** Plain starting gear on the racks, free: a rusty
sword, a club, a dagger, a leather cap and jerkin, a shield, a torch, a
backpack, and the notice board. `world.respawn_room` is here.

**The Long Hall (2).** The old soldier (209) teaches bash and twin for
coin; the herald (212) is the questmaster and sells for quest points.
East is the Good god's wayside shrine (4, a temple of the Good god),
whose shrine-keeper (214) sells Bless to any apostle, a sun-disk and a
cudgel to anyone, and tells a new player what the god wants: the sun
medallion Fewmaster Toede took from Solace's shrine (solace 338). Up a
stair is the hedge-wizard's loft (14), where the hedge-wizard (215)
teaches the simple common spells to anyone with the Intelligence and
the coin (RULES 6.1). North is the Hall of Flickering Windows (13), to
the three early levelling areas; west the Hall of Old Tales (12).

**Below.** A stair under the armory goes down (5): north from its foot
the old road climbs to Solace, and past Solace run the Haven Road and
the Towers of High Sorcery. Beyond, the Undercroft of Doors (6) and the
halls beneath it open on the rest of the world (see `data/README.md`).
