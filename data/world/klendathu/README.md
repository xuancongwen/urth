# klendathu

Klendathu, the bug homeworld, on the day the Mobile Infantry dropped
on it: Fire Base Whiskey (a drop-ship pad, a compound, a supply bunker,
a briefing bunker with a Federal Network screen that asks if you would
like to know more, and a drill ground under Career Sergeant Zim), then
the wire, and beyond it the bug planet: a killing field, chewed-stone
spires, a gully of boots, a canyon and roost of hoppers, a burrow field,
the hive mounds, a downed drop ship, Rasczak's overrun ridge bunker,
a tanker's burned trench, and a battery of plasma bugs shelling the
Fleet. Then the Great Bug Hole and the tunnels under it, down to the
Brain Bug's cavern. Levels 16 to 42, rising from the wire outward and
downward. Reached north from the Hall of Distant Stars (room 10 in
`start`), which opens onto the drop-ship pad (1700).

Bosses: the tanker bug (32) in its trench, and the Brain Bug (42, 20000
health, a psychic scream that hits everyone near it every round,
regeneration) at the bottom of the tunnels. Named carriers: Lieutenant
Rasczak (28, legless and dying in the ridge bunker; his sidearm), the
tanker bug (32; the Mobile Infantry helmet, in its gut), the Brain Bug
(42; the Roughnecks' Morita).

Levels, in walking order: warrior bugs 16, blooded warrior bugs 18,
elder warrior bugs 20 (swarms, all `assist`), digger bugs 22, hopper
bugs 24 (they `wander`), burrower bugs 26, Rasczak 28, tunnel warrior
bugs 30, the tanker bug 32 (fire spray that sets you burning, a shell
that soaks blows), plasma bugs 36 (slow, half a swing a round, and
every swing a heavy one), royal guard bugs 40, the Brain Bug 42.

Uniques: Lt. Rasczak's sidearm (28; crits, finishes the wounded, takes
less damage near death, Charisma and Constitution), the Mobile
Infantry helmet (32; sees bugs dug into the walls, wards, Constitution
and Wisdom), the Roughnecks' Morita (42; an underslung grenade now and
then, crits, dodge, Dexterity and Strength). Common finds: warrior bug
leg claws (20), hopper leg spikes (24), royal guard mandibles (40);
jump boots (22), ceramic greaves (26), a slab of tanker shell (32),
plasma-scorched gauntlets (36), and a Federation heavy assault suit
(42) drop from kills. A tactical nuke charge, timer at zero, lies in
the sapper's gallery (flavor; it is very heavy).

The supply bunker and the briefing bunker are safe. Everything from
the throat of the Great Bug Hole down (1719 to 1726) is dark: the
quartermaster sells field lights, a trooper on the wire carries a
spare, and one lies at the lip of the hole.

Merchants (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- Quartermaster Sergeant Okafor (1701, supply bunker 1702): Morita
  magazine 5, field light 25 (or two carapace plates), MI body armor
  300, Morita Mk I rifle 400; Morita Mk III assault rifle for 600 silver
  plus three carapace plates; bug-plate cuirass for 500 plus two
  carapace plates and two hopper wings; TW-203 flamethrower for 700
  plus a tanker fire gland and a hopper wing.
- Private Ace Levy (1721, drill ground 1704): his fiddle (held, 20;
  Charisma and Dexterity) for 250 silver.

Carapace comes from every kind of warrior bug, diggers, and burrowers;
hopper wings from hoppers; fire glands from the tanker bug; plasma
charge sacs (worth 90 to any merchant) from plasma bugs.

Trainers: Career Sergeant Zim (1700, drill ground 1704) teaches bash
and rescue; Johnny Rico (1719, 32, at the lip of the Great Bug Hole
1715) teaches second wind; Colonel Carl Jenkins (1703) teaches feint;
Pilot Carmen Ibanez (1705, at her drop ship 1700) teaches dodge.

People, in walking order: Pilot Carmen Ibanez (20), Lieutenant
Zander Barcalow (22, her flight instructor) and a FedNet correspondent
on the drop-ship pad; the quartermaster; Colonel Carl Jenkins (30) in
the briefing bunker; Zim (30), Ace Levy (22) and troopers on the drill
ground; Private Dizzy Flores (24), troopers and a neo-dog on the wire;
and Johnny Rico (32) at the lip of the Great Bug Hole, about to go
down. All are peaceful but Zander (silver and a field light) and Dizzy
(her Morita and body armor), who fight back and can be killed for what
they carry.

Quest item: 1719, a trooper's dog tags (planted by questmasters only).
