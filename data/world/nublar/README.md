# nublar

Isla Nublar, from Jurassic Park: a theme park of living dinosaurs on an
island off Costa Rica, a week before opening and a night after the
fences failed. The great gates and the visitor center (rotunda, control
room, gift shop, game warden's lodge) stand at the east end; the tour
road runs west past the compy gully, the gallimimus valley, the
dilophosaur, triceratops, and parasaur paddocks, the brachiosaur
meadow, the aviary, and the raptor pen, through the paddock gate to the
tyrannosaur paddock and down to a river where something that is not on
the map lives. Levels 4 to 40, rising westward. Reached through the
west gates of hub room 9 in `start`, which open onto the great gates
(2400).

Bosses: the raptor matriarch (26) on her nest, the spinosaurus (32) at
the river landing, and the Tyrannosaurus rex (40), which wanders the
paddock and the far road (2425 to 2429). The closed paddock gate (2419
west) keeps her there until someone leaves it open. Named carriers:
Dennis Nedry (12, the Barbasol can), the clever girl (24, Muldoon's
shotgun), the raptor matriarch (the raptor claw dagger), the
Tyrannosaurus rex (her tooth), and John Hammond (peaceful; he holds his
amber-topped cane, and trades it for the Barbasol can).

Uniques: Muldoon's shotgun (crits, finishes the wounded, Dexterity and
Wisdom), Hammond's amber-topped cane (held; Charisma, Wisdom,
regeneration), the can of Barbasol shaving cream (held; Intelligence
and Dexterity; Hammond's price for the cane), the raptor claw dagger
(crits that hit harder, dodge, Dexterity), the Tyrannosaurus rex tooth
(crits, finishes the wounded, Strength and Constitution). Common finds:
hiking boots on the compys, a machete in the valley, Nedry's yellow
rain slicker, a cattle prod in the triceratops paddock, keeper's
gauntlets on the aviary catwalk, a raptor handler's helmet in the pen,
and a flashlight in the maintenance shed. Drop only: a ranger's combat
knife (25), a bent jeep roll bar (32), a 10,000-volt warning sign
(shield, 40), a snapped fence post (40).

The rotunda, the gift shop, and the game warden's lodge are safe. The
control room is not: Nedry can be killed for the can. The maintenance
shed and the generator bunker below it are dark; the gift shop sells
flashlights and night-vision goggles, and a flashlight lies in the shed.

Merchants (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- The gift shop clerk (2401, gift shop 2403): flashlight 25,
  night-vision goggles 90, ranger's bush hat 60, InGen ranger's jacket
  150, tranquilizer rifle 380; dinosaur-hide duster for 300 silver plus
  three strips of hide; raptor-claw spear for 420 silver plus three
  dinosaur claws; a flashlight for two strips of hide.
- John Hammond (2400, rotunda 2401): his amber-topped cane, for the
  Barbasol can Nedry stole.

Hide comes from the gallimimus, the triceratops, and the parasaurs;
claws from the dilophosaurs, the pteranodons, and the raptors.

Trainer: Robert Muldoon (2402, lodge 2404) teaches trip and hamstring.

Quest item: 2405, a speckled velociraptor egg (planted by questmasters
only).
