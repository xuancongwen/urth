# kurast

Kurast, the jewel of the east gone to jungle, and Travincal, the temple
city of the Zakarum, under which the High Council keeps Mephisto, the
Lord of Hatred: the third act of the Diablo II chain. Reached by sea
from Lut Gholein: Meshif's ship ties up at Kurast Docks (3300), whose
`west` exit goes back to Lut Gholein's docks (`lutgholein`, 3201). At
the bottom of the Durance of Hate, the red portal to Hell burns in
Mephisto's chamber (3301), and its `down` exit goes to the Pandemonium
Fortress (`pandemonium`, 3400). Levels 36 to 46.

The docks (3300, 3302 to 3307) are safe. The spider caves, the Flayer
Dungeon, the Ruined Temple, the sewers, and everything in the Durance
of Hate are dark; Meshif sells ship's lanterns at the pier.

Levels, roughly in walking order: giant spiders 36 (assist; the Spider
Forest and the Arachnid Lair), Sszark the Burning 38 (the Spider
Cavern; Khalim's Eye), thorned hulks 38 (the Great Marsh; thorns),
flayers 39 (assist; the Flayer Jungle and Dungeon), Stormtree 40 (the
Flayer Jungle; Stormshield), flayer shamans 40 (assist; fire), Witch
Doctor Endugu 41 (the Flayer Dungeon; the Gidbinn and Khalim's Brain),
Zakarum zealots 41 (assist; Lower Kurast, the bazaar, Upper Kurast,
the causeway), Battlemaid Sarina 42 (the Ruined Temple; Lam Esen's
Tome), Icehawk Riftwing 42 (the sewers; Khalim's Heart and Frostburn),
council members 43 (assist; the causeway and Travincal), Toorc Icefist
and Geleb Flamefinger 43 (Travincal), Ismail Vilehand 44 (the council
hall; Khalim's Flail), blood lords 44 (assist, leech; the Durance),
Bremm Sparkfist, Wyand Voidbringer and Maffer Dragonhand 45 (the third
level of the Durance; Natalya's Shadow, Soul and Totem), and Mephisto
46.

The giant spiders, thorned hulks, flayers and their shamans, the
Zakarum zealots, the council members, and the blood lords attack on
sight; the named elders and the minibosses wait to be challenged.

Boss: Mephisto, the Lord of Hatred (46) in his chamber (3301): 9000
health, two swings a round, a cold blast that chills, an aura of
lightning, and soaks blows. He carries his red soulstone and the
Oculus. Minibosses, with health set: Sszark 2200 (ignites), Stormtree
2600 (lightning procs, thorns), Endugu 2800 (ignites), Sarina 3000
(crits), Icehawk 3000 (chills), Toorc 3200 (chills), Geleb 3200
(ignites), Ismail 4000 (soaks), Bremm 4000 (lightning procs), Wyand
4000 (leech), Maffer 4000 (ignites).

Uniques: the Gidbinn (41; a poisoned ritual dagger, Intelligence and
Wisdom, regeneration), Stormshield (40; blocks, wards, lightning back
at attackers), Frostburn (42; gauntlets, Intelligence and Wisdom,
dodge), Ormus' Robes (42; a dusk shroud, Intelligence and Wisdom, an
aura of fire, frost and sparks), Khalim's Flail (44; a little holy
lightning), Khalim's Will (44; a flail that cracks with lightning,
hits harder, Strength and Wisdom), Natalya's Totem, Shadow and Soul
(45; the Viz-jaq'taar harness: head, body, feet; Dexterity,
Constitution, dodge, wards, regeneration), Natalya's Mark (46; a claw,
crits, hits harder, sees the invisible, Dexterity), the Oculus (46,
held; sees the hidden and the invisible, Intelligence and Wisdom,
soaks blows, arcane spells stronger and harder to resist). Common finds: spider-silk wraps on the spiders, jungle
machetes (drop), hulk-hide jerkins, flayers' barbed spears, zealots'
war scepters, lacquered Kurast helms (drop), shrunken-head wands on the flayer
shamans (3340; 40, harmful spells stronger, Intelligence, mana), council
robes and Zakarum censers (3341; 40, held, divine spells stronger, mana
in a fight, Wisdom), blood
lords' war axes, Durance plate cuirasses (drop), greatswords of the
Durance (drop).

Quest materials: Khalim's Eye (Sszark), Brain (Endugu), Heart
(Icehawk), and Flail (Ismail) go to Hratli; Lam Esen's Tome (Sarina)
goes to Ormus; Mephisto's soulstone goes to Natalya.

  healing 45.
- Natalya (3305, the stockade gate 3305): Natalya's Mark for
  Mephisto's soulstone plus 1500 silver.

Trainers: Natalya teaches feint and backstab. Ormus (3301, his
shelter 3304) teaches arcane spells to those who have the school:
globe of invulnerability, cloudkill, chain lightning, mislead, circle
of death, and disintegrate. All six townsfolk trade or teach, and all
are peaceful.

Scrolls (`study`, found spells no trainer sells): Geleb Flamefinger
(3318, room 3321) carries a scroll of meteor swarm (3335), which
Ormus's look hints at; a scroll of incendiary cloud (3336) is caught
in Sszark's web in her nest (3310), as Sszark's look says.

Quest item: 3333, a jade figurine (planted by questmasters only).
