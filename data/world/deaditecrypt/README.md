# deaditecrypt

The Deadite Crypt, from Army of Darkness: the burial vaults under a
medieval graveyard where the Necronomicon Ex-Mortis was laid to rest,
and where somebody said the words wrong. Skeleton hordes drill in the
halls, deadites crawl off their shelves, flying heads cackle in the
dark, a witch keeps house at the bottom of a pit, and Evil Ash holds
the book at the altar. A farming area: levels 14 to 18, with Evil Ash
at 19, crowded rooms of skeletons that `assist` one another, a
eight-minute reset, and plain gear at every level from 13 to 20 so every
kill can roll loot. Reached down a ladder from the graveyard above
(room 2110 in `barovia`); 2700 is the crypt mouth. Deadites and flying
heads attack on sight; the skeletons wait to be hit.

Boss: Evil Ash (19) at the Necronomicon's altar. Named carriers: the
Pit witch (18, Ash's chainsaw hand) in her hollow, and the skeleton
general (17, Ash's boomstick) in the barracks of the dead.

Uniques: the Necronomicon Ex-Mortis (held; a rotting aura, life leech,
Intelligence and Wisdom), Ash's boomstick (a two-handed shotgun: crits,
finishes the wounded), Ash's chainsaw hand (one hand; a
roaring second bite, crits, Strength). Common finds: a rusted gladius
and cracked bone greaves (13), notched skeleton swords and a rotted
leather jerkin (14), a bone-handled mace (on the barracks rack), rusted
mail coifs and grave-mouldered boots (15), skeleton archers' longbows,
a deadite's grave shroud, and bone bracers (16), captains' broadswords,
skull-crested helms, and a shield of boards and bones (17), a witch's
bone knife, a corroded plate cuirass, and a sword belt (18), a
castle-forged longsword, steel gauntlets, and a barred great helm (19),
and a deadite-slaying war axe and a castle mail hauberk (20). Skeletons
carry yellowed skulls; deadites carry black claws.

The wise man's alcove (2701) is safe and lit. The crypt mouth is lit by
the hole above. Everything from the crypt stairs down is dark; the wise
man sells pitch torches, and one lies at the foot of the ladder.

Merchants (`list`, `buy`, `sell`):

- The wise man (2700, alcove 2701): pitch torch 15 silver, or one
  yellowed skull; bone bracers for 250 silver plus two skulls; a castle
  mail hauberk for 450 silver plus three skulls and two deadite claws.

Trainer: Lord Arthur (2701, alcove 2701) teaches bash and rescue.

Quest item: 2706, a page torn from the Necronomicon (planted by
questmasters only).
