# enterprise

The Starship Enterprise, after the original Star Trek: a ship in orbit
with tribbles in every corridor and Klingons in Engineering and on the
bridge, and below it an away-team world with mugato on the ridges,
Horta guarding their eggs in the mining tunnels, and the Gorn on the
desert where a starship captain once built a cannon out of bamboo.
Levels 7 to 11, with the Gorn captain at 12: an early levelling area,
crowded rooms, a two-minute reset, and loot that is mostly what the
drop rolls give. Reached north from the Hall of Flickering Windows
(room 13, north of the Long Hall) through the humming panels into the
transporter room (4200); `down` from there beams to the planet (4207),
and `up` from the beam-down point beams back.

Rising from the entrance: tribbles (7) all over decks five and six, a
great many of them, which neither assist nor attack; mugato (8, a
venomous bite) in the canyon and on the ridge; Klingon boarders (9) at
the turbolift, in Engineering, and on the bridge; Klingon warriors (10)
in Engineering and on the bridge; Horta (10, acid; resist acid, poison,
and pierce) in the tunnels and the egg vault; Gorn warriors (10) on the
desert and in the boulders; Gorn hunters (11) in the boulders and the
camp. Klingons, mugato, and Gorn attack on sight.

Boss: the Gorn captain (12, `boss`, 4214). Named: Kor, the Klingon
commander (11, Main Engineering 4205).

Uniques, from loot tables (not worn, so not seen before the fight):
Kirk's bamboo cannon (the Gorn captain, one time in five; two-handed,
pierce and fire, a blast-of-diamonds proc, Wisdom) and Kor's d'k tahg
(Kor, one time in four; dagger, crits, Strength).

Common finds: nothing is carried. Every kill may roll one piece of
plain drop gear, one or two at each level from 6 to 12: velour uniform
tunic (6); type-1 hand phaser and Starfleet boots (7); Klingon baldric
and Klingon blade (8); mugato-horn spear, Klingon disruptor pistol,
environment suit helmet (9); type-2 phaser pistol and Klingon battle
tunic (10); Klingon gauntlets and Gorn stone club (11); phaser rifle
and Gorn-hide cuirass (12).

Crew, peaceful: Mr. Scott at the transporter (4200, safe), Dr. McCoy
in sickbay (4203), and a security officer in a red shirt at the
beam-down point (4207).
