# pandora

The Arid Badlands of Pandora, after Borderlands: Fyrestone at the end
of the bus line, a scrap yard, the skag gully and its den, the Dahl
Headlands and the rakk cliffs, a bandit camp, Bone Head's hideout, the
salt flats, and Nine-Toes' walled skag pit and arena. Levels 1 to 5,
with Nine-Toes at 6: an early levelling area, crowded rooms, a
eight-minute reset, and loot that is mostly what the drop rolls give.
Reached west from the Hall of Flickering Windows (room 13, north of
the Long Hall) through the corrugated iron door onto the Fyrestone bus
stop (4000).

Rising from the entrance: skag pups (1) in Fyrestone and the scrap
yard; rakks (2) on the crane, the headlands, and the cliffs; bandits
(2) at the Dahl camp and the shacks; skags (3) on the road, in the
gully, and in the den; spitter skags (4, acid) in the den, on the
salt flats, and in the pit; psychos (4) round the shacks, the trailers,
and the flats; bruisers (5) with Bone Head and at the gate; alpha skags
(5) in the den, at the gate, and in the pit. Skags, spitters, rakks,
psychos, and alphas attack on sight; everything but the townsfolk
assists.

Boss: Nine-Toes (6, `boss`, 4014), with his pet skags Pinky and Digit
(5). Named: Bone Head (5, his hideout 4011).

Uniques, from loot tables (not worn, so not seen before the fight):
T.K.'s Wave (Nine-Toes, one time in five; two-handed shotgun, pierce,
a skipping-pellet proc, Dexterity) and Bone Head's mask (Bone Head,
one time in four; face, Strength, crits).

Common finds: nothing is carried. Every kill may roll one piece of
plain drop gear, one or two at each level from 1 to 6: scavenged
bandit pistol and dusty bandana (1); rusty machete and skag-hide vest
(2); bandit-made repeater and skag-hide boots (3); scrap buzz axe,
padded bandit jacket, tyre-tread gloves (4); bandit scattergun,
scrap-plate helmet, car-door shield (5); Dahl combat rifle and
scrap-plate chestpiece (6).

Townsfolk, peaceful: Claptrap (4000), Dr. Zed (4001), T.K. Baha
(4015). The bus stop and T.K.'s shack are safe.
