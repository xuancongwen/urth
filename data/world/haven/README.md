# haven

The Haven Road, north of Solace in the war of the Lance: the road out
past the fields, the crossroads called Gateway, the occupied town of
Haven with its gutted abbey of the Seekers, and the east road over
Darken Ford to the edge of Wayreth Forest. Levels 10 to 16. Reached from
Solace's North Road (309).

The road belongs to the Dragonarmy: baaz draconians patrol it (11, in
pairs, and they assist), kapaks poison their blades (13), hill ogres hold
the ford (14), and an officer of the Blue Dragonarmy keeps the gate (15).
The boss is the bozak priest (16) in the abbey, for a dark medallion
(neck, Wisdom and divine spell power).

Safe: the Trough and Tankard and the red-robed scrivener's stall. The
scrivener (14, peaceful) sells a robe of red wool and a traveller's staff,
and tells the way into Wayreth: east past the ford, follow the trees that
lean toward the dawn, and bring the Conclave's summons, which a renegade
took in the forest.

The crossroads is meant to grow: the roads to Palanthas and to the coast
for Ergoth start here.
