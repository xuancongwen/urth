# underdark

The Underdark below Icewind Dale's mines: wild tunnels, a chasm, a drow
road, and a shrine to Lolth at the bottom, with the deep-gnome city of
Blingdenstone as the one safe place. Levels 10 to 18, the hardest area
so far. There is no door to the hub: the only way in is down the shaft
from room 640 in `icewind` (the entry room 700 has `up: 640`). Most of
the tunnels are dark; bring a light, or buy the lamp at the hostel.

Levels, roughly in walking order: goblin slaves 8, rothe 9, cave fisher
10, quaggoth 12, hook horror 13 (boss of the chasm), drow warriors and
wizard 14, shrine spiders 15, drow priestess 18 (the boss; the whip,
the signet, the necromancy totem, and the offering are with her).
