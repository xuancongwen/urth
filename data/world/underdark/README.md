# underdark

The Underdark below Icewind Dale's mines: wild tunnels, a chasm, a drow
road, and a shrine to Lolth at the bottom, with the deep-gnome city of
Blingdenstone as the one safe place. Levels 10 to 20, the hardest area
so far. There is no door to the hub: the only way in is down the shaft
from room 640 in `icewind` (the entry room 700 has `up: 640`). Most of
the tunnels are dark; bring a light, or buy the lamp at the hostel.

Levels, roughly in walking order: goblin slaves 10, rothe 10, cave fisher
10, quaggoth 12, hook horror 13 (boss of the chasm), drow warriors 16,
drow wizard 17, shrine spiders 18, drow priestess 20 (the boss; the whip,
the signet, the insignia of House Do'Urden, the necromancy totem, and
the offering are with her).

Notable items: Icingdeath lies among the bones at the chasm floor
(room 717, level 14); the drow wizard keeps Twinkle as a prize (17);
Zaknafein's sword is Lolth's trophy in the antechamber (722, 19). The priestess's
snake-headed whip poisons and slows.
