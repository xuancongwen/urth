# cowlevel

The Secret Cow Level, which does not exist: moonlit fields under a
bruise-coloured sky, a cow pen, a farmstead that burns and never burns
down, a ring of standing stones, the herd's war camp, and the Cow
King's throne field, all of it full of Hell Bovines. Levels 20 to 26,
and the Cow King at 27. A farming area: a tight level band, crowded
rooms, a two-minute reset (`interval_seconds: 120`), and plain drop
gear at every level from 19 to 28, so every kill can roll loot.

Reached from Tristram: west of the graveyard wall (diablo room 1308)
hangs a shimmering red portal, locked and pickproof. Its key is Wirt's
leg (2800), which Wirt (diablo mob 1305, on his crate in 1308) sells
for 500 silver. The portal opens onto the portal landing (2800).

Boss: the Cow King (27; 1400 health, a crack of
lightning, soaks blows) on his throne field (2813). He wears all three
uniques.

Uniques, the Cow King's leathers (a set in name; each piece stands
alone):

- the Cow King's horns (head): thorns, Strength and Constitution
- the Cow King's hide (body): Constitution, regeneration, soaks blows
- the Cow King's hooves (feet): Dexterity, Strength, dodge

Mobs, all flagged assist, in walking order: hell bovines 20 (the field
edge and the grass), hell bovine archers 21, bruisers 22 (the cow pen
and the farmstead), stray hell bovines 22 (wander), halberdiers 23
(the farmstead, the barn, the open field), reavers 24 (the meadow
ring), champions 25 (the trampled field and the war camp), war
champions 26 (the champions' ground, the camp, the throne field).
About 50 bovines stand in the fields after a full reset.

Bruisers, reavers, and war champions attack on sight; the rest of the
herd waits to be started, though the assist flag brings it in.

Common finds (rolled loot only, flagged drop, two or three a level):
19 notched bovine halberd, cowhide jerkin; 20 hell bovine halberd,
cowhide boots, horn-capped club; 21 hayfork, cowhide leggings, bovine
longbow; 22 bovine war axe, cowhide gauntlets, bovine plate helm; 23
great halberd, bovine plate greaves, cowhide hood; 24 bovine poleaxe,
bovine plate cuirass, cowhide bracers; 25 champion's halberd, bovine
plate gauntlets, hide-covered shield; 26 champion's war maul, bovine
plate sabatons and vambraces; 27 king's guard halberd, bovine war
plate; 28 gilded bovine halberd, gilded bovine helm, cowhide
pauldrons.

The portal landing (2800) is safe. Nothing is dark: the moon is too
big for that. There are no merchants past the portal.

Quest item: 2801, a dented cowbell (planted by questmasters only).
