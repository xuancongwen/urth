# ff7

Final Fantasy VII: Midgar and the Planet. The Sector 7 slums under the
plate, with Seventh Heaven (Tifa's bar, and Barret and AVALANCHE
downstairs) and the night train to Mako Reactor 1, where the Guard
Scorpion waits on the core gantry; Sector 5 and Aerith's church; Wall
Market, Don Corneo's mansion and Aps in the sewer below it; the Sector 7
pillar with Reno at the top; the Shinra Building, floor by floor
(Hojo's laboratory with Red XIII in his cage, Jenova BIRTH out of its
tank, the President's office with Heidegger and Scarlet, and Rufus on
the roof); then out along the Midgar expressway to the grasslands,
Kalm, the marsh and the Midgar Zolom, the Mythril Mine and Elena, the
Gold Saucer and its Battle Square, Cosmo Canyon and the Cave of the Gi,
Nibelheim and the Shinra Mansion with Vincent Valentine in his coffin,
Rocket Town and the launch pad (Palmer and Rude), Wutai and Yuffie on
the Da-Chao, the Temple of the Ancients (Tseng and the Red Dragon), the
Forgotten Capital and the altar where Aerith prayed (Jenova LIFE), the
Great Glacier, and the Northern Crater: Jenova SYNTHESIS in the depths
and Sephiroth at the Center of the Planet. Levels 20 to 60. Reached
east from the Hall of Far Futures (room 11 in `start`) through the
door stamped SHINRA, which opens onto the Sector 7 train station
(3700).

Raid boss: Ultima Weapon (60, 51000 health), on its roost across the
crater rim (3742), is meant for a full group of about seven, not for
one hero. It carries the Knights of the Round materia, whose falling
blades strike everyone fighting it each round and whose Ultimate End
lands on one; a slowing grip of the Planet's wrath; a leech in its
bracer of the Lifestream; and thirteen more pieces of armour the Planet
grew it, which turn most blows. Its own innate effects are modest (a
slowing chill and a steady footing); its gear carries most of its
power, and the Ultima Weapon sword rides in its body unwielded, so its
blows are its own natural Ultima Beam. A lone level-60 fighter lasts
about six rounds and barely marks it. In a group simulation of
level-60 fighters in standard kit (retuned 2026-10-12), seven won
about half the time and eight nearly always.

Final boss: Sephiroth (59, 32000 health, boss), with the Masamune and
his black coat, at the Center of the Planet: a fight for a party. A
lone level-59 fighter lasts about five rounds and deals about a third of
his health.
