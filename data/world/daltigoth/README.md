# daltigoth

Southern Ergoth and the ruins of Daltigoth, where the White Order kept its
Test under the crater of its lost Tower (abjuration and evocation; RULES
6.1). Levels 18 to 26: the farthest of the three Towers. Reached from
Palanthas: east of the Great Square, the harbour (6400), and the
Perechon under Maquesta Kar-Thon (peaceful) to the ice shore.

Thanoi walrus-men hold the shore (19, ivory harpoons), an ice bear
the glacier road (20), and ogres the ruins (21, hide jerkins). The
ogre chieftain of Daltigoth (23, boss, 1200 health) sits in the broken
palace wearing the white seal, the key to the slab over the hidden stair
in the crater where the Tower stood (pickproof). Everything on the way
and in the Test attacks from level 1.

Under the crater, safe: the undercroft, the library (the keeper of the
white shelves teaches the White Order's school spells to its sworn
specialists), and the white robing room (the white robe and a staff of
white wood). Below is the Test: shadow hounds after a child (20),
an ogre magus of the Lost Battles (22), and the Kingpriest's templar (24,
boss, white mail and a gold circlet). Past them, the Chamber of the Test
(6415, `order: white`), where Par-Salian (26, peaceful) hears the oath:
`swear abjuration` or `swear evocation`.
